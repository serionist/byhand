# Signs of AI writing: German, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- de-dive-in (weak; post, document): Invites the reader to dive in with it, or offers to break the subject down, as a chatbot's walk-through does. Instead: Start with the first point. Words: lass uns eintauchen: cut; lass mich es … aufschlüsseln: give the parts; taucht ihr ein: cut
- de-em-dash (weak): Sets off a clause with the long English dash (—); the spaced short dash (–) is German's own, and a person's. Instead: Use a comma or a full stop, or the spaced short dash.
- de-emphasis-phrases (weak; post, document): Tells the reader that a point deserves attention before making it. Instead: Make the point; the reader decides what matters. Words: es ist wichtig, zu betonen: cut, and give the fact
- de-future-closers (weak; post, document): Closes on a stock look ahead that promises nothing anyone can check. Instead: End on the last fact, or the next date. Words: darf man gespannt sein: say what is decided, and when; die zukunft gestalten, gestalten wir die zukunft: say what you will do next; die zukunft sieht … rosig aus: give the plan or the forecast; nur die zeit wird zeigen: cut; spannende zeiten liegen: cut
- de-hedging (weak; post, document): Softens plain claims with kann and könnte throughout, or stacks two hedges on one claim. Instead: State it, or name the one condition that makes it uncertain.
- de-list-announcers (weak; post, document): Announces a list with a stock phrase of plenty before naming the items; common in people's ad copy too. Instead: Name the items, or give the number. Words: eine vielzahl an: viele, or the number; eine breite palette an: name the items
- de-participle-asides (weak): Hangs a present participle on a sentence to say what the fact shows, a form German prose seldom uses. Instead: Stop at the fact; if its meaning matters, give it a sentence of its own. Words: verdeutlichend, widerspiegelnd, gewährleistend, unterstreichend, symbolisierend, hervorhebend, betonend: cut
- de-promo-words (weak; post, document): Sells a place, a firm or a product with brochure words where a neutral one would do. Instead: Describe what is there, and let the reader judge. Words: atemberaubend…: say what one sees; bahnbrechend…: say what is new, and since when; im herzen der: in, or the address; kulturelle teppich…: name the groups; paradigmenwechsel…: say what changed for whom; reiche erbe: name one piece of it; revolutionier…: say what it changes; wegweisend…: say who followed
- de-rhetorical-openers (weak; post, document): Opens with a question nobody asked: what if, have you ever wondered. Instead: Start with the point or the news.
- de-significance-phrases (weak; post, document): Dresses a plain subject in set phrases of legacy and deep meaning. Instead: Give the fact or the number that shows it matters. Words: bleibende erbe: say what remains; dient als zeugnis: zeigt; fasziniert bis heute: say who still comes or reads it; schlüsselmoment…: give the date and what changed; tief verwurzelt…: say since when, or cut; unerschütterlich…: say what they kept doing; unterstrich die … rolle: zeigt, or the fact itself
- de-sowohl-triad (weak; post, document): Stretches sowohl … als auch over three items: a rule of three in German dress. Instead: List the items with und, or keep the two that matter.
- de-stiff-connectives (weak): Starts sentence after sentence with ferner, des Weiteren or darüber hinaus; one of them, or a paper full of them, is a person's. Instead: Use und or auch, or let the order of the sentences carry the link.
- de-stock-openers (weak; post, document): Opens on a stock line about fast-changing modern times. Instead: Start with the subject. Words: im zeitalter der ki: cut; in der heutigen, modernen welt: cut, or name the year; in einer sich wandelnden welt: cut
- de-title-case-headings (weak): Capitalises every word of a heading the English way, articles, und, für and adjectives included. Instead: Capitalise the first word, nouns and names only.
