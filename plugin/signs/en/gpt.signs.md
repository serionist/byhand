# Signs of AI writing: English, GPT

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- en-copula-avoidance (weak; gpt 4 and later): Avoids plain 'is' and 'has' with grander verbs. Instead: Write 'is' or 'has'. Terms: `boasts an?`: has; `functions as an?`: is; `holds the distinction of being`: is; `serv(?:ed|es|ing) as an?`: is; `stands as an?`: is
- en-em-dash (weak; gpt 3.5 to 5.0): Uses spaced em dashes as pivots, more than a person writing the same kind of text. Instead: Use a comma, a colon, brackets or a full stop.
- en-gpt-2023-vocabulary (weak; gpt 3.5 to 4.x): Uses the words of 2023 to mid-2024 ChatGPT. Instead: Use the plain word. Terms: `boasts`: has; `bolster(?:ed|ing|s)?`: support; `enduring`: lasting; `garner(?:ed|ing|s)?`: got; `intricac(?:ies|y)`: details; `meticulous(?:ly)?`: careful; `testament`: proof
- en-gpt-2024-vocabulary (weak; gpt 4 to 5.x): Uses the words of mid-2024 to mid-2026 ChatGPT. Instead: Use the plain word. Terms: `emphasi[sz]ing`: stressing; `enhanc(?:e|ed|es|ing)`: improve; `highlighting`: showing; `showcas(?:e|ed|es|ing)`: shows
- en-gpt-5-vocabulary (weak; gpt 5 to 5.x): Stresses media coverage and online presence, as GPT-5 does. Instead: Say what the source says, not what kind of source it is. Terms: `independent coverage`: name the report; `(?:local|national|regional) media outlets`: name them; `(?:maintains?|has) an? (?:active|strong) (?:digital|online|social media) presence`: cut; `trade publications`: name them
- en-gpt-6-vocabulary (weak; gpt 6 and later): Uses GPT-6's plainer words. Instead: Prefer the exact word. Terms: `dependable`: reliable; `echoed`: say who repeated it; `prioriti[sz](?:e|ed|es|ing)`: put first; `universally`: say who
- en-nominalisation (weak; gpt 4 to 4.x): Packs sentences with nouns made from verbs. Instead: Turn the noun back into a verb, with a named subject.
