# Signs of AI writing: Russian, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- ru-title-case-headings (usual): Capitalises every word of a heading, an English habit; Russian capitalises the first word and names only. Headings set in capitals throughout, as contracts use, are not this. Instead: Capitalise the first word and proper names; leave и, в, на and the rest small.
- ru-calques (weak; post, document, deck): Pairs a Russian verb and noun the way English pairs them, not the way Russian does. Instead: Use the Russian pairing: решить проблему, успеть к сроку. Words: адресует бол…: решить; встрет… дедлайн…: успеть к сроку; доставит ценность: say what the user gets
- ru-challenges-formula (weak): Ends on a stock pair: difficulties named in general, then a bright future. Instead: Name the problem and what is being done about it, or stop. Words: будущее выглядит ярким: give the next step; несмотря на все вызовы: cut; продолжа… процветать: give a number; столкну… с рядом вызов…: name the problem
- ru-chat-residue (weak; post, document, deck): Leaves a chat assistant's sign-off: a hope that it helped, an offer of more. Instead: Delete it; end on the last useful fact. Words: буду рад… помочь: cut; если хотите узнать что-то еще: cut; надеюсь, это поможет: cut
- ru-chopped-fragments (weak; post, document): Breaks a point into a run of verbless scraps for effect. Instead: Write one full sentence with its verb.
- ru-dangling-participle (weak): Starts on a деепричастие whose doer never turns up as the subject. Instead: Name the doer as the subject.
- ru-didactic-phrases (weak): Tells the reader what is worth noting before saying it. Instead: Say it; the reader decides what matters. Words: крайне важно отметить: cut; стоит обратить внимание на то: cut
- ru-math-signs (weak; post, document): Puts =, >, < or vs between words in running prose. Instead: Write the relation out in words.
- ru-office-register (weak; post, document): Writes a post or an article in office register: осуществлять, является, данный, в целях, strings of genitives. Letters, minutes, contracts and reports use it by custom; leave them. Instead: Use the plain verb and этот, and say who does what.
- ru-paragraph-stack (weak; post, document): Links paragraphs only with кроме того or также, so none grows out of the one before. Instead: Let each paragraph answer, extend or turn on the one before it.
- ru-poster-maxim (weak; post, document): Puts a claim as a two-noun maxim; the dash in it is plain Russian, the maxim is the sign. Instead: Say what actually happened, with a number if there is one.
- ru-promotional-words (weak; post, document, deck): Describes a place or a business in tourist-brochure words. Instead: Say where it is and what is there. Words: … могут похвастаться: есть; богат… культурн… прошл…: name what is there; в самом сердце: в центре, or the address; захватывающ… дух: describe it; потрясающ… красот…: describe it
- ru-significance-phrases (weak): Hangs symbolism and turning points on ordinary facts with set phrases. Instead: Say what happened; name the change only if you can. Words: служ… символом: say what it shows; знамену…: начинается, or give the date; неизгладим… след…: say what changed; непоколебим…: cut; поворотн… этап…: give the date and the change; символизир…: say who reads it so
- ru-stock-openers (weak): Opens with a line about the modern world or what everyone knows. Instead: Start with the subject. Words: в современном мире: cut; каждый из нас: say who; не секрет, что: cut
- ru-tacked-on-participles (weak): Ends a sentence on a participle clause that draws a moral from the fact. Instead: Stop at the fact, or give the cause in a sentence of its own. Words: демонстрируя: cut; отражая: name the cause; подчеркивая: cut, or say who stresses it; способствуя: say what it changed
- ru-therapy-register (weak; post, document, deck): Comforts the reader with stock lines of emotional support. Instead: Give the fact that would reassure, or cut. Words: вы не одиноки: say who else has it; давайте признаем: cut; и это окей: cut; позвольте себе: cut
- ru-throat-clearing (weak; post, document, deck): Spends a sentence on what it is about to say, or on how frank it is being. Instead: Start with the point. Words: без лишних слов: cut; вот в чем фокус: cut; вот что важно знать: cut; давайте начистоту: cut; давайте разберемся: cut; если копнуть глубже: cut; настоящий вопрос в том: ask it; погрузимся в: cut
- ru-vague-authority (weak): Leans on unnamed experts and analysts. Instead: Name the person or the study, or drop the claim. Words: исследователи утверждают: name them; некоторые критики: name them
- ru-verb-echo (weak): Reuses the last sentence's verb for the next sentence's new subject. Instead: Give each subject the verb that fits it; repeat one only for emphasis.
