A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A pattern is what Grep runs on a file, case-insensitive and line by line; a sign with none is found by reading.

- ar-narrow-vocabulary (weak; post, document): Circles a few common words, repeating them more than people do, and uses fewer of a field's own terms, names and places. Instead: Use the field's own term, the name and the place, and let a plain word come once.
- ar-register-drift (weak; message, post): In a text meant in one dialect, mixes in another dialect's forms, or slides back into standard Arabic for whole sentences. Instead: Keep the dialect the text opens in; a standard term, a set phrase or a quote is how people write too.
- ar-shed-light (weak; post, document): Says that a text sheds light on its subject (تسليط الضوء, يسلط الضوء على); one alone is ordinary Arabic, in news above all. Instead: Say what it shows: the finding, the name or the number.
