# Signs of AI writing: English, Llama

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more in one paragraph.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- en-llama-phrases (weak; llama 4 and later): Uses Llama 4's business-essay phrases. Instead: Say what was done and what came of it. Terms: `gain(?:s|ing)? a deeper understanding`: learn; `stay(?:s|ing)? ahead of the competition`: name the rival; `far-reaching consequences`: name them; `role in shaping`: say what it changed; `continues? to evolve`: say what changed
- en-nominalisation (weak; llama 3 to 3.x): Packs sentences with nouns made from verbs. Instead: Use the verb and say who acts.
