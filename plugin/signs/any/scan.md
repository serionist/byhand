# Signs of AI writing: every language, every model, to scan a file

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A pattern, in backticks after a sign's brackets, is what Grep runs on a file, case-insensitive and line by line.

- any-chat-tool-markup (strong): `\[attached_file:\d+\]|\[web:\d+\]|ppl-ai-file-upload|:::writing\{variant=`
- any-chatbot-url-tag (strong): `utm_source=(?:chatgpt\.com|openai|copilot\.com|claude\.ai|perplexity\.ai)|referrer=grok\.com`
- any-chatgpt-citation-markup (strong; gpt 4 and later): `contentReference\[oaicite|oaicite:\d|oai_citation|attributableIndex|turn\d+(?:file|image|news|search)\d+`
- any-deepseek-citation-markup (strong; deepseek 1 and later): `【\d+†`
- any-gemini-citation-markup (strong; gemini 1 and later): `\[span_\d+\]\((?:end|start)_span\)|\[cite: ?\d+(?:, ?\d+)*\]`
- any-grok-citation-markup (strong; grok 1 and later): `grok_render_citation_card_json|grok_card|<grok-card\b`
- any-emoji-markers (usual; email, document): `^\s*(?:#{1,6}\s+[\U0001F300-\U0001FAFF✅❌✨⚡⭐]|(?:[-*•–]|\d+[.)])\s+(?:[\U0001F300-\U0001FAFF✨⚡⭐]|[✅❌]\s*\*\*[^*\n]+(?::\*\*|\*\*\s*[:：])))`
- any-unfilled-placeholder (usual): `\[(?:add|company|describe|insert|recipient|your)\b[^\]\n]*\](?:[^(]|$)|\b(?:insert|paste)_[a-z0-9_]+_here\b|20\d\d-xx-xx`
- any-bold-lead-in-list (weak): `^\s*(?:[-*•–]|\d+[.)])\s+\*\*[^*\n]+(?::\*\*|\*\*\s*[:：])|^\*\*\d+[.、]`
- any-raw-markdown (weak; email, post, document): `\*\*[^*\n]+\*\*|^#{2,4} \S|^(?:-{3,4}|\*{3}|_{3})\s*$`
