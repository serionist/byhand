A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- vi-article-opener (weak; post, document): Opens by telling the reader what the article or section will do together with them (Trong bài viết này, chúng ta sẽ cùng…). Instead: Start with the first fact; the title already says what the text is about. Words: ở phần này, chúng ta sẽ: cut
- vi-emphasise (weak; document): Says that a text or a result emphasises something (nhấn mạnh), where it could say the thing; ordinary in a report of what a speaker stressed. Instead: Give what it shows: the number, the decision or the date. Words: nhấn mạnh: give what it shows
