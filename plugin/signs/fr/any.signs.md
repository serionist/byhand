# Signs of AI writing: French, every model

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- fr-ai-vocabulary (weak; post, document): Uses the rare, grand words French model output is full of: holistique, synergie, pierre angulaire, véritable before an abstract noun. A single one proves nothing. Instead: Name what is actually there: the two teams, the shared planning, the street. (?:atout|révolution|levier|pilier|moteur|tournant)s?`: drop véritable
- fr-article-rituals (weak; post, document): Talks about the article instead of writing it: announces its plan, invites the reader to dive in or explore together. Instead: Cut the tour-guide lines; the headings already show the plan. Terms: `dans (?:cet|ce) (?:article|billet|guide),? (?:nous allons|je vais|on va)`: cut; `(?:plongeons|explorons|découvrons)(?: ensemble| dans)`: cut; `sans plus (?:attendre|tarder)`: cut; `vous l['’]aurez compris`: cut; `entrons dans le vif du sujet`: cut
- fr-article-scaffold (weak; post, document): Lays a post out like a template web page: question headings, teaser headings (Ce que…), a summary list up top, an FAQ, a list of sources at the end. Instead: Title sections by what they say, and attach each source to the claim it backs.
- fr-calques (weak; post, document): Uses business English turned into French verbs: adresser un enjeu, délivrer de la valeur, naviguer dans l'incertitude. Instead: Use the idiom French already has for it. Terms: `adress(?:er|e|ent|ons|ez|é|ée|ées) (?:un|l['’]|le|ce|cet|les|des|ces) ?(?:enjeux?|défis?)`: traiter; `délivr(?:er|e|ent|ons|ez|ant) (?:de la |plus de )?valeur`: apporter; `navigu(?:er|e|ent|ons|ez|ant) (?:dans|à travers) (?:la|l['’]|cette|les|ces) ?(?:complexités?|incertitudes?)`: say how
- fr-connector-openers (weak; post, document): Opens most paragraphs on a formal link word, so the text reads as a pile of additions. One now and then is ordinary French. Instead: Let the order of the facts carry the link; keep a connector where the argument turns.
- fr-copula-avoidance (weak; post, document): Avoids a plain être or avoir with frames that place a thing in an approach or a logic: s'articule autour de, s'inscrit dans une démarche, se positionne comme. Instead: Say what the thing is or does, and who does it.
- fr-dash-asides (weak): Encloses aside after aside in spaced em dashes, several to a paragraph; dialogue dashes and an occasional incise are French typography, not this. Instead: Rewrite the sentence so the aside isn't needed, or move it to the next one.
- fr-engagement-lines (weak; post): Frames a social post with stock lines: proud to announce, link in the comments, a closing Et vous ? or an opening Et si… ? Instead: Open with the news, paste the link where it is mentioned, and stop once the news is told.
- fr-english-headings (weak; post, document): Headings in English style over French text: a capital on each word, or an English label such as Key Takeaways. Instead: Case the heading as a sentence, and give an English label its French words.
- fr-false-contrast (weak; post, document): The French forms of a set-up contrast: a first half softened by seulement, juste or simple, or an il ne s'agit pas… mais. Correcting a real mistake is not one. Instead: Give Y its evidence, a figure or an example, instead of a foil.
- fr-filler-notice (weak; post, document): Sets a stock preface before a remark, or says a point needs no proof, in the French phrases below. Instead: Start the sentence at the fact; show the proof or drop the claim. Terms: `il (?:convient|est (?:important|essentiel|crucial|intéressant)) de (?:noter|souligner) qu(?:e|['’])`: cut; `n['’]est plus à (?:démontrer|prouver)`: give the proof
- fr-hashtag-block (weak; post): Closes a post with a run of broad tags that name a mood or a sector, not anything a reader would look up. Instead: Let the post end on its last sentence.
- fr-hedge-stack (weak): Softens one claim twice over (pourrait potentiellement), so the reader can't tell how likely it is. Instead: Pick the verb that matches the odds (peut, risque de, va); in a negotiation, leave the writer's own care alone.
- fr-inflated-stakes (weak; post, document): French stock phrases of eras, turning points and a bright future. Instead: Report the event at its own size: what opened, closed or moved, and when. Terms: `tournants? (?:décisifs?|historiques?)`: say what changed; `moments? charnières?`: give the date; `(?:nouvelle|prochaine) ère`: say what starts; `à l['’]ère (?:du numérique|du digital|de l['’]ia|de l['’]intelligence artificielle)`: cut; `dans le paysage (?:actuel|numérique|digital)`: name the market; `aujourd['’]hui plus que jamais`: cut; `l['’]avenir s['’]annonce (?:prometteur|radieux)`: end on the fact; `(?:seul )?l['’]avenir nous le dira`: end on the fact; `plus (?:motivée?s?|déterminée?s?) que jamais`: say what comes next
- fr-performative-candour (weak; post, document): Promises honesty or straight talk before saying something quite ordinary. Instead: Start at the statement; if it is candid, the reader will see it. Terms: `soyons (?:honnêtes|franc(?:he)?s|lucides)`: cut; `pour être (?:tout à fait )?(?:honnêtes?|transparente?s?|franc(?:he)?s?)`: cut; `sans langue de bois`: cut; `la vérité,? c['’]est que`: cut
- fr-slogan-mirrors (weak; post, document): Writes ad-copy fragments in matched pairs: a short Moins de… sentence and then a Plus de… one, or two Ni… and then Juste… Instead: Write one plain sentence with the real numbers in it.
- fr-staged-reveal (weak; post, document): Builds a small drumroll in front of an ordinary fact: a promised reason, a twist, a question nobody asked. Instead: Cut the teaser; the sentence after it already holds the point. - fr-stock-openers (weak; post, document): Opens a text or a paragraph on how fast the world moves, before any fact about the subject. Instead: Begin with the news itself.
