# Signs of AI writing: Hungarian, Grok

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- hu-hyphenated-case-ending (usual; grok 4 and later): Joins a case ending to a Hungarian name that ends in a or e with a hyphen and no long vowel (Anna-val), which a native speaker doesn't write. Instead: Join the ending to the name and lengthen its last vowel: Annával, Bencének, Nyíregyházán.
- hu-place-name-suffix (usual; grok 4 and later): Gives a well-known Hungarian town the wrong place ending (Szegedben for Szegeden, Győrön for Győrben), which a native speaker doesn't write. Instead: Use the town's own ending: Szegeden, Pécsen, Miskolcon, Kecskeméten; but Győrben, Debrecenben, Egerben, Sopronban.
