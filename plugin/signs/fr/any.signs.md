# Signs of AI writing: French, every model

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- fr-ai-vocabulary (weak; post, document): Uses the rare, grand words French model output is full of: véritable before an abstract noun, pierre angulaire, synergie, holistique. A single one proves nothing. Instead: Name what is actually there: the two teams, the shared planning, the street. Terms: `niché(?:es|e|s)?`: give the place; `véritables? (?:moteur|pilier|tournant|levier|atout|révolution)s?`: drop véritable; `façonn(?:ées|és|ée|é|ant|ent|er|e)`: say how; `pierres? angulaires?`: base; `synergies?`: say who does what together; `insuffl(?:ées|és|ée|é|ant|ent|er|e)`: say what changes; `holistiques?`: say what is included
- fr-article-rituals (weak; post, document): Talks about the article instead of writing it: announces its plan, invites the reader to dive in or explore together. Instead: Cut the tour-guide lines; the headings already show the plan. Terms: `entrons dans le vif du sujet`: cut; `vous l['’]aurez compris`: cut; `sans plus (?:tarder|attendre)`: cut; `(?:explorons|plongeons|découvrons)(?: dans| ensemble)`: cut; `dans (?:ce|cet) (?:guide|billet|article),? (?:on va|je vais|nous allons)`: cut
- fr-article-scaffold (weak; post, document): Lays a post out like a template web page: question headings, teaser headings (Ce que…), a summary list up top, an FAQ, a list of sources at the end. Instead: Title sections by what they say, and attach each source to the claim it backs.
- fr-calques (weak; post, document): Uses business English turned into French verbs: adresser un enjeu, naviguer dans l'incertitude. Instead: Use the idiom French already has for it. Terms: `navigu(?:ant|ons|ent|ez|er|e) (?:à travers|dans) (?:ces|cette|les|la|l['’]) ?(?:incertitudes?|complexités?)`: say how; `délivr(?:ant|ons|ent|ez|er|e) (?:plus de |de la )?valeur`: apporter; `adress(?:ées|ée|é|ons|ent|ez|er|e) (?:ces|cet|ce|des|les|l['’]|le|un) ?(?:défis?|enjeux?)`: traiter
- fr-connector-openers (weak; post, document): Opens most paragraphs on a formal link word, so the text reads as a pile of additions. One now and then is ordinary French. Instead: Let the order of the facts carry the link; keep a connector where the argument turns.
- fr-copula-avoidance (weak; post, document): Avoids a plain être or avoir with reflexive frames that set a thing inside an approach, around axes, or in a role. Instead: Say what the thing is or does, and who does it.
- fr-dash-asides (weak): Encloses aside after aside in spaced em dashes, several to a paragraph; dialogue dashes and an occasional incise are French typography, not this. Instead: Rewrite the sentence so the aside isn't needed, or move it to the next one.
- fr-engagement-lines (weak; post): Frames a social post with stock lines: proud to announce, link in the comments, a closing Et vous ? or an opening Et si… ? Instead: Open with the news, paste the link where it is mentioned, and stop once the news is told.
- fr-english-headings (weak; post, document): A French heading that looks translated: capitals all along the line, or a section label such as Key Takeaways still in English. Instead: Retype it as a French memo would set it: Notre bilan du premier trimestre.
- fr-false-contrast (weak; post, document): The French forms of a set-up contrast whose first half is played down with seulement, juste or simple. Correcting a real mistake, or scoping a plan, is not one. Instead: Give Y its evidence, a figure or an example, instead of a foil.
- fr-filler-notice (weak; post, document): The French form of a didactic aside: an impersonal il convient or il est important clause in front of the point itself. Instead: Begin the sentence where the clause after que begins. Terms: `il (?:est (?:intéressant|essentiel|important)|convient) de (?:souligner|noter) qu(?:e|['’])`: cut
- fr-hashtag-block (weak; post): Closes a post with a run of broad tags that name a mood or a sector, not anything a reader would look up. Instead: Let the post end on its last sentence.
- fr-hedge-stack (weak): Softens one claim twice over (pourrait potentiellement), so the reader can't tell how likely it is. Instead: Pick the verb that matches the odds (peut, risque de, va); in a negotiation, leave the writer's own care alone.
- fr-inflated-stakes (weak; post, document): French stock phrases of eras, turning points and a bright future. Instead: Write it as a status update would: what happened, and on which date. Terms: `plus (?:déterminée?s?|motivée?s?) que jamais`: say what comes next; `(?:seul )?l['’]avenir nous le dira`: end on the fact; `l['’]avenir s['’]annonce (?:radieux|prometteur)`: end on the fact; `aujourd['’]hui plus que jamais`: cut; `dans le paysage (?:digital|actuel|numérique)`: name the market; `à l['’]ère de l['’](?:intelligence artificielle|ia)`: cut; `(?:prochaine|nouvelle) ère`: say what starts; `moments? charnières?`: give the date; `tournants? (?:historiques?|décisifs?)`: say what changed
- fr-performative-candour (weak; post, document): Promises honesty or straight talk before saying something quite ordinary. Instead: Start at the statement; if it is candid, the reader will see it. Terms: `la vérité,? c['’]est que`: cut; `sans langue de bois`: cut; `pour être (?:tout à fait )?(?:franc(?:he)?s?|transparente?s?|honnêtes?)`: cut; `soyons (?:lucides|franc(?:he)?s|honnêtes?)`: cut
- fr-slogan-mirrors (weak; post, document): Writes ad-copy fragments in matched pairs: a short Moins de… sentence and then a Plus de… one, or two Ni… and then Juste… Instead: Write one plain sentence with the real numbers in it.
- fr-staged-reveal (weak; post, document): Builds a small drumroll in front of an ordinary fact: a reason promised for the next sentence, a twist still to come. Instead: Cut the teaser; the sentence after it already holds the point. Terms: `la question n['’]est (?:pas|plus) de savoir si`: state the point; `mais ce n['’]est pas tout`: cut; `et voici (?:comment|pourquoi)`: give the reason; `c['’]est (?:ici|là) qu['’]entre(?:nt)? en (?:scène|jeu)`: say what it does; `c['’]est (?:ici|là) qu(?:['’]|e )[^.!?\n]{1,40} entre(?:nt)? en (?:scène|jeu)`: say what it does; `c['’]est (?:ici|là) que (?:les choses|cela|ça) devien(?:nent|t) intéressant(?:es)?`: cut
- fr-stock-openers (weak; post, document): Opens a text or a paragraph on how fast the world moves, before any fact about the subject. Instead: Begin with the news itself.
