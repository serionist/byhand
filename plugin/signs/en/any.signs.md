# Signs of AI writing: English, every model

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- en-stock-openers (usual): Uses a stock line about the modern world, or about unlocking potential. Instead: Start with the subject. Terms: `in today['’]?s (?:digital|ever-changing|fast-paced|modern) (?:age|era|landscape|world)`: cut; `navigat(?:e|es|ing) the complexit(?:ies|y)`: say what the task is; `unlock(?:ing|s)? the (?:potential|power)`: say what becomes possible
- en-ai-vocabulary (weak): Leans on the same few elevated words; one is chance, several together are not. Instead: Use the plain word, or the fact the word stands in for. Terms: `aligns? with`: matches; `complexities`: problems; `crucial`: say why it matters; `deep dive`: close look; `delv(?:e|ed|es|ing)`: look at; `foster(?:ed|ing|s)`: build; `interconnectedness`: links; `interplay`: how A affects B; `intricate`: detailed; `let['’]?s dive in(?:to)?`: cut; `leverag(?:e|ed|es|ing)`: use; `multifaceted`: name the parts; `nuanced`: say what the nuance is; `pivotal`: say why it matters; `robust`: say how it holds up; `seamless(?:ly)?`: smooth; `tapestry`: mix; `transformative`: say what changed; `underscor(?:ed|ing)|underscores? (?:a|an|how|its|our|that|the|their|this)`: shows; `vibrant`: lively
- en-even-sentences (weak): Keeps every sentence about the same length and shape. Instead: Let length follow the thought, some short and some long.
- en-significance-phrases (weak): Reaches for set phrases that inflate a subject's importance. Instead: Say what happened and to whom. Terms: `enduring legacy`: say what remains; `evolving landscape`: name the market or field; `indelible mark`: say what changed; `(?:is|serves as|stands as) a testament to`: shows; `mark(?:ed|ing|s)? a (?:pivotal|significant) (?:moment|shift)`: give the date and the change; `play(?:ed|ing|s)? an? (?:crucial|pivotal|vital) role`: say what it does; `reflects? broader`: name the trend, or cut; `setting the stage for`: led to; `valuable insights`: say what was learnt
- en-stiff-verbs (weak): Picks the stiff verb where a person uses the short one. Instead: Use the short verb. Terms: `attempted`: tried; `authored`: wrote; `relocated`: moved; `utili[sz](?:e|ed|es|ing)`: use
- en-vague-connection (weak): Joins two things with a loose word of connection and never says how. Instead: Give the actual relation: who did what, and where.
