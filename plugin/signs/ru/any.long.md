# Signs of AI writing: Russian, every model, for posts and documents

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- ru-calques (weak; post, document, deck): Pairs a Russian verb and noun the way English pairs them, not the way Russian does. Instead: Use the Russian pairing: решить проблему, успеть к сроку. Words: адресует бол…: решить; встрет… дедлайн…: успеть к сроку; доставит ценность: say what the user gets
- ru-chat-residue (weak; post, document, deck): Leaves a chat assistant's sign-off in a post or a document: a hope that it helped, an offer of more. Instead: Delete it; end on the last useful fact. Words: буду рад… помочь: cut; если хотите узнать что-то еще: cut; надеюсь, это помог: cut
- ru-chopped-fragments (weak; post, document): Breaks a point into a run of verbless scraps for effect. Instead: Write one full sentence with its verb.
- ru-math-signs (weak; post, document): Puts =, >, < or vs between words in running prose. Instead: Write the relation out in words.
- ru-office-register (weak; post, document): Writes a post or an article in office register: осуществлять, является, данный, в целях, strings of genitives. Letters, minutes, contracts and reports use it by custom; leave them. Instead: Use the plain verb and этот, and say who does what.
- ru-paragraph-stack (weak; post, document): Links paragraphs only with кроме того or также, so none grows out of the one before. Instead: Let each paragraph answer, extend or turn on the one before it.
- ru-poster-maxim (weak; post, document): Puts a claim as a two-noun maxim; the dash in it is plain Russian, the maxim is the sign. Instead: Say what actually happened, with a number if there is one.
- ru-promotional-words (weak; post, document, deck): Describes a place or a business in tourist-brochure words. Instead: Say where it is and what is there. Words: ё могут похвастаться: есть; богат… культурн… прошл…: name what is there; в самом сердце: в центре, or the address; захватывающ… дух: describe it; потрясающ… красот…: describe it
- ru-therapy-register (weak; post, document, deck): Comforts the reader with stock lines of emotional support. Instead: Give the fact that would reassure, or cut. Words: вы не одиноки: say who else has it; давайте признаем: cut; и это окей: cut; позвольте себе: cut
- ru-throat-clearing (weak; post, document, deck): Spends a sentence on what it is about to say, or on how frank it is being. Instead: Start with the point. Words: без лишних слов: cut; вот в чем фокус: cut; вот что важно знать: cut; давайте начистоту: cut; давайте разберемся: cut; если копнуть глубже: cut; настоящий вопрос в том: ask it; погрузимся в: cut
