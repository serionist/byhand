# Signs of AI writing: Finnish, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A pattern is what Grep runs on a file, case-insensitive and line by line; a sign with none is found by reading.

- fi-em-dash (weak): Sets off a clause with the long English dash (—), where Finnish writes the spaced short dash (–). Instead: Use a comma, a colon or a full stop, or the spaced short dash. Pattern: `[^\s—.,] ?— ?[^\s—]`
- fi-title-case-headings (weak; document, deck, post): Capitalises every word of a heading, as English titles do; Finnish capitalises only the first word and names. Instead: Capitalise the first word and names: ## Kevään koulutukset Tampereella
