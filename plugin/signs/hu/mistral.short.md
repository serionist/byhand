# Signs of AI writing: Hungarian, Mistral, for every kind of writing

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- hu-hyphenated-case-ending (usual; mistral 3.1 and later): Joins a case ending to a Hungarian name that ends in a or e with a hyphen and no long vowel (Anna-val), which a native speaker doesn't write. Instead: Join the ending to the name and lengthen its last vowel: Annával, Bencének, Nyíregyházán.
- hu-place-name-suffix (usual; mistral 3.1 and later): Says a thing is in a town that takes -en, -on or -ön with -ban or -ben instead (Szegedben for Szegeden), which a native speaker doesn't write. Instead: Use the town's own ending: Budapesten, Kecskeméten, Miskolcon, Pécsen, Szegeden. Leave alone what is right: a club's, a county's or a hotel's name (Hilton Budapestben, Jász-Nagykun-Szolnokban), a verb that takes -ban (csalódtam Budapestben), and the town's other endings (Győrön keresztül).
