# Signs of AI writing: Spanish, every model, for every kind of writing

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- es-dash-pivots (weak): Uses the long dash the English way, as a pivot: spaced on both sides, or joined to the words on both sides. Spanish opens a raya before an aside and closes it after, and starts dialogue with one. Instead: Use a colon, a comma or a full stop; keep the raya for a real aside or dialogue.
- es-mixed-address (weak): Switches between tú and usted, or between imperative and infinitive steps, within one text. Instead: Keep one form of address for the reader and one form for the steps.
- es-service-script (weak; email, message): Stacks the customer-service script: a team working hard, an offer to get in touch, regret for the inconvenience, thanks for patience and understanding. Each alone is a person's set phrase. Instead: Say what happened, the new date and when you will write again; keep one closing.
