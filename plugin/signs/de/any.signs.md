# Signs of AI writing: German, every model

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- de-dive-in (weak; post, document): Invites the reader to dive in with it, or offers to break the subject down, as a chatbot's walk-through does. Instead: Start with the first point. Terms: `tauch(?:en|t) (?:wir|sie|ihr) (?:\w+ ){0,3}ein`: cut; `lass(?:t|en sie)? uns (?:\w+ ){0,3}eintauchen`: cut; `lass(?:en sie)? mich (?:das|dies|es) (?:\w+ )?aufschlüsseln`: give the parts
- de-em-dash (weak): Sets off a clause with the long English dash (—); the spaced short dash (–) is German's own, and a person's. Instead: Use a comma or a full stop, or the spaced short dash.
- de-emphasis-phrases (weak; post, document): Tells the reader that a point deserves attention before making it. Instead: Make the point; the reader decides what matters. Terms: `bemerkenswert ist,? dass`: cut, and give the fact; `es ist (?:wichtig|entscheidend|wesentlich) zu (?:betonen|beachten|bedenken|bemerken)`: cut; `es sei (?:darauf )?hingewiesen`: cut, or bitte beachten; `es ist anzumerken`: cut
- de-english-constructions (weak): Builds German sentences the English way: an idiom, a preposition or a word order carried over. Instead: Write it the way you would say it in German.
- de-future-closers (weak; post, document): Closes on a stock look ahead that promises nothing anyone can check. Instead: End on the last fact, or the next date. Terms: `nur die zeit wird (?:es )?zeigen`: cut; `(?:wir dürfen|man darf|darf man) gespannt sein`: say what is decided, and when; `die zukunft sieht (?:\w+ )?(?:rosig|vielversprechend|glänzend) aus`: give the plan or the forecast; `spannende zeiten (?:stehen|liegen)`: cut; `(?:die|unsere) zukunft (?:gemeinsam )?(?:zu )?gestalten|gestalten wir (?:gemeinsam )?(?:die|unsere) zukunft`: say what you will do next
- de-hedging (weak; post, document): Softens plain claims with kann and könnte throughout, or stacks two hedges on one claim. Instead: State it, or name the one condition that makes it uncertain.
- de-list-announcers (weak; post, document): Announces a list with a stock phrase of plenty before naming the items; common in people's ad copy too. Instead: Name the items, or give the number. Terms: `eine[rn]? vielzahl an`: viele, or the number; `eine[rn]? (?:breite|große|umfangreiche)n? palette (?:an|von)`: name the items
- de-participle-asides (weak): Hangs a present participle on a sentence to say what the fact shows, a form German prose seldom uses. Instead: Stop at the fact; if its meaning matters, give it a sentence of its own. Terms: `(?:verdeutlichen|widerspiegeln|gewährleisten|unterstreichen|symbolisieren|hervorheben|betonen)d`: cut
- de-promo-words (weak; post, document): Sells a place, a firm or a product with brochure words where a neutral one would do. Instead: Describe what is there, and let the reader judge. Terms: `atemberaubend\w*`: say what one sees; `bahnbrechend\w*`: say what is new, and since when; `im herzen (?:der|des|von)`: in, or the address; `revolutionier\w*`: say what it changes; `reiche[nrs]? (?:kulturelle[nrs]? )?(?:erbe|geschichte|tradition)`: name one piece of it; `wegweisend\w*`: say who followed; `paradigmenwechsel\w*`: say what changed for whom; `kulturelle[nrs]? teppich\w*`: name the groups
- de-rhetorical-openers (weak; post, document): Opens with a question nobody asked: what if, have you ever wondered. Instead: Start with the point or the news.
- de-significance-phrases (weak; post, document): Dresses a plain subject in set phrases of legacy and deep meaning. Instead: Give the fact or the number that shows it matters. Terms: `tief ?verwurzelt\w*`: say since when, or cut; `unterstr(?:eicht|eichen|ich|ichen) (?:die|seine|ihre|deren|dessen) (?:\w+ )?(?:bedeutung|rolle|relevanz)`: zeigt, or the fact itself; `unerschütterlich\w*`: say what they kept doing; `schlüsselmoment\w*`: give the date and what changed; `(?:steht|stehen|stand|standen|dient|dienen|diente|dienten) als zeugnis`: zeigt; `bleibende[ns]? (?:erbe|vermächtnis)`: say what remains; `fasziniert (?:bis heute|weiterhin|nach wie vor)`: say who still comes or reads it
- de-sowohl-triad (weak; post, document): Stretches sowohl … als auch over three items: a rule of three in German dress. Instead: List the items with und, or keep the two that matter.
- de-stiff-connectives (weak): Starts sentence after sentence with ferner, des Weiteren or darüber hinaus; one of them, or a paper full of them, is a person's. Instead: Use und or auch, or let the order of the sentences carry the link.
- de-stock-openers (weak; post, document): Opens on a stock line about fast-changing modern times. Instead: Start with the subject. Terms: `in einer sich (?:\w+ ){0,2}(?:wandelnden|verändernden) welt`: cut; `in (?:der|unserer) heutigen,? (?:schnelllebigen|digitalen|vernetzten|modernen) (?:zeit|welt|gesellschaft)`: cut, or name the year; `im (?:digitalen )?zeitalter (?:der|von) (?:digitalisierung|ki|künstlicher intelligenz)`: cut
- de-summary-formulas (weak; post, document): Announces a wrap-up with a set formula, then restates what came before. People write these too. Instead: Stop at the last new point, or state the conclusion plainly. Terms: `es bleibt festzuhalten`: cut; `(?:abschließend|insgesamt|zusammenfassend|alles in allem) lässt sich (?:sagen|festhalten|feststellen)`: cut, and state the point; `abschließend sei (?:noch )?(?:erwähnt|gesagt|angemerkt)`: cut; `es lässt sich festhalten`: cut
- de-title-case-headings (weak): Capitalises every word of a heading the English way, articles, und, für and adjectives included. Instead: Capitalise the first word, nouns and names only.
