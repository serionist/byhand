# Signs of AI writing: Korean, Gemini

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- ko-gemini-colon-headings (weak; gemini 2.5 and later; document, post): Gives heading after heading a label, a colon and a subtitle. Instead: Use one plain heading that says what the section says.
- ko-gemini-hype (weak; gemini 2.5 and later): Reaches for Gemini's hype adjectives: explosive, sweeping, overwhelming. Instead: Give the figure: the share, the count, the change. Terms: `폭발적\w*`: give the number; `대대적\w*`: say how many; `압도적\w*`: give the share; `파격적\w*`: give the rate; `막강(?:한|하|했)\w*`: say how big
- ko-gemini-shift-slogan (weak; gemini 2.5 and later): Sums up a change as a slogan from one quoted label to another, 'X'에서 'Y'로. Instead: Say what changed, for whom, and when.
