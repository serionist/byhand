# Signs of AI writing: Chinese, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- zh-holding-space (usual; gpt 5.4 and later; claude 4.7 and later): Comforts like a counsellor: promises to hold the reader steady, and tells them what they really feel. Instead: Answer what was said; offer help in plain words, and don't diagnose.
- zh-aphorism (weak): Ends a point on a coined maxim that adds no fact: the real X is Y, or X is Z's Y. Instead: Replace it with the concrete claim, or leave it out.
- zh-chat-residue (weak): Keeps chat lines meant for the person who asked: hope this helps, copy it as is, ask me anything else. Instead: Cut them; end with the text itself.
- zh-connective-stacking (weak; post): Starts sentence after sentence with 此外, 然而, 与此同时 or 因此, where the order already links them. Instead: Drop most of them; keep one only where it carries a turn or a cause.
- zh-dash-density (weak): Leans on dashes (——) to bolt clauses together, several in one paragraph. Instead: Join clauses with ordinary punctuation; one dash a paragraph at most.
- zh-decorative-detail (weak; post, document): Sets a scene with made-up particulars that nothing later in the text depends on. Instead: Cut them; keep a detail only if it is real and the story needs it.
- zh-era-opener (weak): Spends its first sentence on the age we live in, or on how fast a field is growing, before the subject appears. Instead: Begin with what happened, when, and to whom.
- zh-first-next-last (weak): Lays any topic out in three even steps marked 首先, 其次 and 最后, whatever the logic. Instead: Follow the logic: two points if there are two, joined by what links them.
- zh-gpt-5-register (weak; gpt 5 and later; claude 5 and later): Talks about ordinary work in debugging and combat verbs, and pushes offers to do more. Instead: Use the plain verb; offer more once, and say what. Words: 揪出来: found; 补一刀: add; 稳稳兜住: handle; 不瞎猜: say what you know; 坐实: confirmed; 我立马开始, 我立馬開始: cut; 收口: finish; 要不要我顺手: offer once, plainly
- zh-honesty-preface (weak; gpt 5.4 and later; claude 4.7 and later): Announces that it is about to be honest or serious, to borrow trust for an ordinary line. Instead: Say the point; let the content carry it. Words: 说句实话, 坦白讲: cut; 我必须认真地说: cut
- zh-leaked-request (weak; document): Leaves the chatbot's request for input inside the text, such as asking for the passage to translate. Instead: Delete it, and check what the tool really returned there.
- zh-lyrical-abstract (weak): Gives feelings and ideas a poetic texture they don't need, with soft or glowing words where a plain one would do. Instead: Say the thing plainly; keep these words for real objects. Words: 滚烫: say what it is; 丰盈: full; 安放: put, or cut; 微光: say what it is; 褶皱: say what it is
- zh-mixed-metaphors (weak): Jumps between unrelated images in one paragraph, a flood, then a machine, then a finishing line, instead of saying what is meant. Instead: Say it literally; keep one metaphor only if it explains something.
- zh-officialese (weak): Inflates a plain action into a padded one, such as 进行了深入的分析 for analysed. A notice's fixed wording, such as 对单据进行审核, is not this. Instead: Use the verb itself, with who did it.
- zh-significance-words (weak): Props up a plain fact with set words of significance, legacy and role. Instead: Say what happened, to whom and when; drop the word if no fact stands behind it. Words: 不断变化的格局: name the market; 不可磨灭: say what remains; 奠定基础: say what it made possible; 意义重大: say what it means; 扮演…角色: say what it does; 标志着, 標誌著: say what changed; 根植于: comes from; 深远影响: name the effect; 至关重要, 举足轻重: say why it matters; 见证了, 見證了: say what happened
- zh-social-media-register (weak; post): Piles up social-media hype words, as a post written in bulk does. Instead: Keep one if it is how you talk; say what the post gives. Words: 划重点: cut; 绝绝子, 谁懂啊: cut; 一文读懂: cut; 保姆级: detailed; 姐妹们: cut; 干货, 乾貨: cut; 建议收藏: cut
- zh-staged-drama (weak): Stages drama: a run of one-phrase fragments, a teasing question before the point, or a canned pause before a reveal. Instead: Write ordinary sentences with a subject; keep one short line at most.
- zh-stock-openers (weak): Opens with a stock signpost that a point matters, or announces what comes next, instead of saying it. Instead: Cut the signpost and start with the point. Words: 废话不多说: cut; 众所周知: cut, or name the source; 不难发现, 不可否认的是: cut; 不言而喻, 毋庸置疑: cut; 值得注意的是, 需要指出的是: cut; state the point; 接下来我将为你, 接下來我將為你, 让我们一起来看看, 讓我們一起來看看: start with it
- zh-translationese (weak): Reads like English carried into Chinese: long chains of 的, stacked 被 passives, and frames like 对于……而言 or 基于. Instead: Break the chain into short clauses with a subject, in the active voice.
- zh-uplift-ending (weak): Closes on an upbeat line about the future, or a call to embrace change together. Instead: Stop after the last fact; if something comes next, name it. Words: 让我们一起拥抱: name the next step; 拭目以待: cut; 未来可期: cut
- zh-wrap-up-phrases (weak): Restates the point under a stock wrap-up, or claims to reach its essence, adding nothing. Instead: Stop at the last new point, or state the claim without the frame. Words: 说到底, 归根结底: cut; 总的来说, 综上所述, 总而言之: cut; 本质上: say the claim; 由此可见: cut
