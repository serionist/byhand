# Signs of AI writing: Japanese, every model, for every kind of writing

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- ja-bold-not-rendered (usual; email, message): Leaves Markdown bold against Japanese brackets, where it shows as bare asterisks. Instead: Drop the asterisks, or put them inside the brackets.
- ja-ai-phrases (weak): Uses stock phrases that leave out who judged, what risk lurks, or who needs the thing. Instead: Name the person, the risk or the use the phrase stands in for. Words: が招く事故: say what goes wrong; との見立てです: name who thinks so; という挙動を排除: say what stops what; に潜む罠, 罠が潜んで: name the problem; 欠かせない存在となって, 今や…欠かせない存在: say who uses it, for what
- ja-em-dash (weak): Sets off asides or trails off with dashes in plain prose, an English habit; in essays and fiction a dash is a person's. Instead: Use 、 and a particle, or end the sentence.
- ja-false-agency (weak): Lets data, culture or problems act on their own: the data tells, a culture is fostered, issues surface. Instead: Name who did it or who noticed. Words: が浮かび上が: で分かった; が浮き彫りに: で分かった; が物語って: を見ると; 文化が醸成: say who built it
- ja-grand-kango (weak): Dresses a small personal experience in grand literary words. Instead: Tell it at its real size, in everyday words. Words: 冷徹な: cold; 境地: say where you got to; 宿命: say why it was bound to happen; 極致: the best; 深淵: depth, or cut; 真理。: say what you found; 禁欲的: plain; 美学: say what you like about it; 虚飾: show
- ja-inflated-endings (weak): Closes on a set tail that overstates or hedges a plain claim. Instead: End on the fact itself. Words: と言えるでしょう: でしょう; に他なりません: です; 過言では: cut, and state the fact
- ja-lesson-endings (weak): Ends each paragraph or small story on a lesson learnt. Instead: Let most end on what happened; draw a lesson once, if at all.
- ja-llm-function-words (weak): Leans, across a whole text, on function words chatbots use several times as often as people: いくつかの, という点, より before an adjective, に関する, かつ. Instead: Keep one where it carries meaning; give the number, or the plain verb.
- ja-pause-comma (weak): Puts a comma straight after a short topic, subject or object, where no reader needs a pause. Instead: Drop it; keep commas after long phrases, conditions and contrasts.
- ja-thin-human-markers (weak): Almost never uses ので, ながら or ば, and seldom quotes anyone. Instead: Use the connectives you would say aloud, and quote people in 「」.
