# Signs of AI writing: every language, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.
A pattern is what Grep runs on a file, case-insensitive and line by line; a sign with none is found by reading.

- any-chat-tool-markup (strong): Leaves a chat tool's file or document tags in the text. Instead: Delete the tag. Pattern: `\[attached_file:\d+\]|\[web:\d+\]|ppl-ai-file-upload|:::writing\{variant=`
- any-chatbot-url-tag (strong): Keeps a link's tracking tag that names a chatbot, which shows one found the link if not the text. Instead: Strip the tag and open the page before you cite it. Pattern: `utm_source=(?:chatgpt\.com|openai|copilot\.com|claude\.ai|perplexity\.ai)|referrer=grok\.com`
- any-chatgpt-citation-markup (strong; gpt 4 and later): Leaves ChatGPT's internal citation tokens or search placeholders in the text. Instead: Delete the token and cite the source, or cut the claim. Pattern: `contentReference\[oaicite|oaicite:\d|oai_citation|attributableIndex|turn\d+(?:file|image|news|search)\d+|gehe zu suche nr`
- any-deepseek-citation-markup (strong; deepseek 1 and later): Leaves DeepSeek's lenticular citation marks in the text. Instead: Delete the mark and cite the source, or cut the claim. Pattern: `【\d+†`
- any-gemini-citation-markup (strong; gemini 1 and later): Leaves Gemini's citation markers in the text. Instead: Delete the marker and cite the source, or cut the claim. Pattern: `\[span_\d+\]\((?:end|start)_span\)|\[cite: ?\d+(?:, ?\d+)*\]`
- any-grok-citation-markup (strong; grok 1 and later): Leaves Grok's citation-card markup in the text. Instead: Delete the markup and cite the source, or cut the claim. Pattern: `grok_render_citation_card_json|grok_card|<grok-card\b`
- any-invented-sources (strong): Cites a book, paper, link or DOI that does not exist or does not say this. Instead: Cite only what you have opened, with the page; otherwise leave the claim out.
- any-knowledge-cutoff (strong; gpt 3.5 to 4.x): Warns that its knowledge stops at a training date. Instead: Check the current fact, or give the date of your source. Pattern: `(?:as of|up to) my last (?:knowledge|training) update|stand meines letzten wissensupdates|bis zu meinem letzten (?:trainings|wissens)-?update|на момент моего обучения|момент (?:последнего )?обновления моей базы данных|мо[её] последнее обновление знаний|à la date de mes connaissances|hasta mi última actualización de conocimientos?\b`
- any-bold-overuse (usual): Bolds many phrases in running text, as a sales sheet would. Instead: Bold almost nothing; put the key point first in the sentence.
- any-challenges-formula (usual): Closes on a set piece: for all its strengths the subject faces challenges, then a vague hopeful line. Instead: Name the real problem and what is being done about it, or stop.
- any-circular-argument (usual): Says the same point again in other words, adding nothing new. Instead: Let each paragraph add a fact or a step; cut the ones that do not.
- any-coverage-name-drop (usual): Proves importance by listing who covered the subject, not what they said. Instead: Report what a source says; name it where that helps the reader.
- any-defensive-qualification (usual): Adds caveats nobody asked for about how far a source goes, aimed at a claim no one made. Instead: Limit a claim only where a reader could overreach, and give the finding.
- any-didactic-aside (usual): Stops to tell the reader which part deserves their attention. Instead: State the point; the reader decides what to note. Pattern: `\bit(?:['’]s| is) (?:crucial|essential|important|worth) (?:noting|to note)|(?:[.!?]\s+|^)\W*fontos (?:(?:azonban|itt|még|ugyanakkor|viszont) )?(?:hangsúlyozni|kiemelni|megjegyezni),? hogy\b`
- any-emoji-markers (usual; email, document): Puts an emoji in front of headings or list items; a ✅ or ❌ ticking off a list item, with no bold label, is a person's. Instead: Drop it; a heading needs no decoration. Pattern: `^\s*(?:#{1,6}\s+[\U0001F300-\U0001FAFF✅❌✨⚡⭐]|(?:[-*•–]|\d+[.)])\s+(?:[\U0001F300-\U0001FAFF✨⚡⭐]|[✅❌]\s*\*\*[^*\n]+(?::\*\*|\*\*\s*[:：])))`
- any-generic-over-specific (usual): Trades a telling detail for praise that would suit anyone. Instead: Keep the detail that fits only this subject: the name, number, date or event.
- any-negative-parallelism (usual): Raises a claim nobody made in order to correct it: not this but that, or not just this. Instead: State the point itself; keep the negation only when someone really holds the view you reject.
- any-overstructured-note (usual; message, email): Breaks a note or a reply into titled sections, as if it were a report. Instead: Write a few plain sentences; use a list only for a real list.
- any-promotional-tone (usual): Reads like a brochure about a place, firm or person. Instead: Describe what is there in neutral words.
- any-significance-inflation (usual): Claims an ordinary fact marks a turning point, a legacy or a wider trend, even one it admits is minor. Instead: State the fact; say what changed only if you can name the change.
- any-source-gap-disclaimer (usual): Says details are scarce in the sources, then writes on as if they were not. Instead: Say plainly what you could not find, and write only what you know. Pattern: `specific details (?:are|remain) (?:limited|scarce)|in the provided (?:search results|sources)|in den bereitgestellten (?:quellen|suchergebnissen)|spezifische details (?:(?:bleiben|sind) )?(?:begrenzt|rar|spärlich)|в предоставленных результатах поиска|(?:ограничен|скудн)\w* в предоставленных источниках`
- any-tacked-on-analysis (usual): Ends a fact on a clause saying what it shows, or has the fact itself highlight or underline something. Instead: Stop at the fact. If its meaning is worth stating, state it separately and name who draws it.
- any-unfilled-placeholder (usual): Leaves a fill-in slot in the text: a bracketed name, a URL stub, a date of XX. Instead: Fill it in or cut it before you send. Pattern: `\[(?:your|insert|add|describe|company|recipient|имя|ваш\w*|вставьте|опишите|укажите|ihre?n?|dein(?:e|en)?|insérer|nom (?:de l['’]entreprise|de la société|du destinataire)|vos coordonnées|votre (?:entreprise|fonction|nom|poste|prénom|société)|insert(?:a|ar|e)|nombre (?:de la|del) (?:cliente|destinatari[ao]|empresa)|su (?:cargo|empresa|nombre)|tu (?:cargo|correo|empresa|nombre|teléfono))\b[^\]\n]*\](?:[^(]|$)|\[[^\]\n]{1,40} (?:einfügen|eintragen|ergänzen)\](?:[^(]|$)|\b(?:insert|paste)_[a-z0-9_]+_here\b|20\d\d-xx-xx|[\[［【]ここに[^\]］】\n]{0,15}(?:入れ|入力|挿入|記入|貼)[^\]］】\n]{0,6}[\]］】](?:[^(]|$)`
- any-vague-authority (usual): Credits a claim to unnamed experts, observers or reports, or makes one source sound like many. Instead: Name the person or document, or drop the claim.
- any-bold-lead-in-list (weak): Starts item after item in a list with a bold label and a colon, or numbers bold paragraph titles. Instead: Write plain items or a sentence; keep labels where a reader scans for them. Pattern: `^\s*(?:[-*•–]|\d+[.)])\s+\*\*[^*\n]+(?::\*\*|\*\*\s*[:：])|^\*\*\d+[.、]`
- any-closing-summary (weak): Ends a section or the whole text by restating it, often under a heading such as Conclusion. Instead: Stop when the point is made; end on the last new thing. Pattern: `^\W*(?:en conclusion|en conclusión|en resumen|in conclusion|in summary|pour conclure|zusammenfassend|в заключение|подводя итог)\b`
- any-false-range (weak): Writes 'from X to Y' with two things that sit on no real scale. Instead: Name the items, or keep only the one you mean.
- any-flattering-agreement (weak): Opens by praising the reader or agreeing with them before it answers. Instead: Answer; disagree where the facts do. Pattern: `you(?:['’]re| are) absolutely right|вы абсолютно правы|這是很好的觀察|这是很好的观察|ha(?:ben sie|bt ihr|st du) absolut recht|(?:ausgezeichnete|großartige|hervorragende|tolle) frage!|¡excelente pregunta!`
- any-letter-boilerplate (weak; email, message): Opens with a stock wish that the message finds the reader well. Instead: Start with the reason you are writing. Pattern: `(?:the|this) (?:email|letter|mail|message) finds you well|(?:diese|meine) (?:e-mail|mail|nachricht) erreicht (?:dich|euch|sie) (?:bei bester gesundheit|wohlauf)|это сообщение застанет вас`
- any-over-attribution (weak; document): Narrates what a record lists or identifies where the fact could stand alone. Instead: State the fact; attribute only what someone could dispute.
- any-raw-markdown (weak; email, post, document): Leaves Markdown symbols such as **, ## or --- rules where they will not render. Instead: Format for where the text lands; in mail and plain text, drop the symbols. Pattern: `\*\*[^*\n]+\*\*|^#{2,4} \S|^(?:-{3,4}|\*{3}|_{3})\s*$`
- any-rule-of-three (weak): Lists three adjectives or short phrases for rhythm, not because there are three things. Instead: Give the items there really are, even if that is one or two.
- any-stock-headings (weak): Titles sections with two nouns joined by 'and', or a stock label such as Legacy. Instead: Use a heading that says what the section says, or none.
- any-synonym-cycling (weak): Renames one thing several ways to avoid repeating a word. Instead: Repeat the plain word.
- any-table-for-prose (weak): Puts a few facts that read better as a sentence into a small table. Instead: Write the sentence; keep tables for data people compare.
