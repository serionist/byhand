# Signs of AI writing: French, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- fr-ai-vocabulary (weak; post, document, deck): Uses the rare, grand words French model output is full of: holistique, pierre angulaire, synergie, véritable before an abstract noun. A single one proves nothing. Instead: Name what is actually there: the shared planning, the street, the two teams. Words: façonne: say how; holistique: say what is included; insuffle: say what changes; niché: give the place; pierre angulaire: base; synergie: say who does what together; véritable atout: drop véritable
- fr-article-rituals (weak; post, document): Talks about the article instead of writing it: announces its plan, invites the reader to dive in or explore together. Instead: Cut the tour-guide lines; the headings already show the plan. Words: dans ce guide, on va: cut; explorons dans: cut; entrons dans le vif du sujet: cut; sans plus tarder: cut; vous l'aurez compris: cut
- fr-article-scaffold (weak; post, document): Lays a post out like a template web page: closing FAQ, question headings, source list at the foot, summary bullets up top, teaser headings (Ce que…). Instead: Title sections by what they say, and attach each source to the claim it backs.
- fr-calques (weak; post, document, deck): Uses business English turned into French verbs. Instead: Use the idiom French already has for it. Words: adresse ce défi: traiter; délivre de la valeur: apporter; navigue dans la complexité: say how
- fr-connector-openers (weak; post, document): Opens most paragraphs on a formal link word, so the text reads as a pile of additions. One now and then is ordinary French. Instead: Let the order of the facts carry the link; keep a connector where the argument turns.
- fr-copula-avoidance (weak; post, document): Avoids a plain être or avoir with reflexive frames that set a thing around axes, in a role or inside an approach. Instead: Say what the thing is or does, and who does it.
- fr-dash-asides (weak): Encloses aside after aside in spaced em dashes, several to a paragraph; an occasional incise and dialogue dashes are French typography, not this. Instead: Rewrite the sentence so the aside isn't needed, or move it to the next one.
- fr-engagement-lines (weak; post): Frames a social post with stock lines: closing on Et vous ?, linking in the comments, opening on Et si… ?, proud announcements. Instead: Open with the news, paste the link where it is mentioned, and stop once the news is told.
- fr-english-headings (weak; post, document, deck): A French heading that looks translated: capitals all along the line, or English section labels such as Wrap-Up. Instead: Retype it as a French memo would set it: Notre bilan du premier trimestre.
- fr-false-contrast (weak; post, document): The French forms of a set-up contrast whose first half is played down with juste, seulement or simple. Correcting a real mistake, or scoping a plan, is not one. Instead: Give Y its evidence, an example or a figure, instead of a foil.
- fr-filler-notice (weak; post, document, deck): The French form of a didactic aside: an impersonal il convient or il est important clause in front of the point itself. Instead: Begin the sentence where the clause after que begins. Words: il convient de noter qu': cut
- fr-hashtag-block (weak; post): Closes a post with a run of broad tags that name a mood or a sector, not anything a reader would look up. Instead: Let the post end on its last sentence.
- fr-hedge-stack (weak): Softens one claim twice over (pourrait potentiellement), so the reader can't tell how likely it is. Instead: Pick the verb that matches the odds (peut, risque de, va); in a negotiation, leave the writer's own care alone.
- fr-inflated-stakes (weak; post, document, deck): French stock phrases of a bright future, eras and turning points. Instead: Write it as a status update would: what happened, and on which date. Words: à l'ère de l'ia: cut; aujourd'hui plus que jamais: cut; dans le paysage actuel: name the market; l'avenir nous le dira: end on the fact; l'avenir s'annonce radieux: end on the fact; moment charnière: give the date; nouvelle ère: say what starts; plus motivé que jamais: say what comes next; tournant décisif: say what changed
- fr-performative-candour (weak; post, document, deck): Promises honesty or straight talk before saying something quite ordinary. Instead: Start at the statement; if it is candid, the reader will see it. Words: la vérité, c'est que: cut; pour être franc: cut; sans langue de bois: cut; soyons francs: cut
- fr-slogan-mirrors (weak; post, document): Writes ad-copy fragments in matched pairs: a short Moins de… sentence and then a Plus de… one, or two Ni… and then Juste… Instead: Write one plain sentence with the real numbers in it.
- fr-staged-reveal (weak; post, document, deck): Builds a small drumroll in front of an ordinary fact: a reason promised for the next sentence, a twist still to come. Instead: Cut the teaser; the sentence after it already holds the point. Words: c'est là qu'entre en jeu: say what it does; c'est là que ça devient intéressant: cut; et voici comment: give the reason; la question n'est pas de savoir si: state the point; mais ce n'est pas tout: cut
- fr-stock-openers (weak; post, document, deck): Opens a text or a paragraph on how fast the world moves, before any fact about the subject. Instead: Begin with the news itself.
