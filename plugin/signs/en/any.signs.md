A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- en-stock-openers (usual): Uses a stock line about the modern world, or about unlocking potential. Instead: Start with the subject. Terms: `in today['’]?s (?:ever-changing|modern|digital|fast-paced) (?:era|landscape|world|age)`: cut; `navigat(?:e|es|ing) the complexit(?:y|ies)`: say what the task is; `unlock(?:s|ing)? the (?:power|potential)`: say what becomes possible
- en-ai-vocabulary (weak): Leans on the same few elevated words; one is chance, several together are not. Instead: Use the plain word, or the fact the word stands in for. Terms: `delv(?:e|es|ed|ing)`: look at; `pivotal`: say why it matters; `crucial`: say why it matters; `multifaceted`: name the parts; `underscor(?:ed|ing)|underscores? (?:the|a|an|its|how|that|this|their|our)`: shows; `foster(?:s|ed|ing)`: build; `interplay`: how A affects B; `interconnectedness`: links; `intricate`: detailed; `complexities`: problems; `nuanced`: say what the nuance is; `tapestry`: mix; `vibrant`: lively; `seamless(?:ly)?`: smooth; `robust`: say how it holds up; `transformative`: say what changed; `aligns? with`: matches; `leverag(?:e|es|ed|ing)`: use; `deep dive`: close look; `let['’]?s dive in(?:to)?`: cut
- en-even-sentences (weak): Keeps every sentence about the same length and shape. Instead: Let length follow the thought, some short and some long.
- en-significance-phrases (weak): Reaches for set phrases that inflate a subject's importance. Instead: Say what happened and to whom. Terms: `play(?:s|ed|ing)? an? (?:crucial|pivotal|vital) role`: say what it does; `(?:is|stands as|serves as) a testament to`: shows; `mark(?:s|ed|ing)? a (?:pivotal|significant) (?:moment|shift)`: give the date and the change; `enduring legacy`: say what remains; `indelible mark`: say what changed; `evolving landscape`: name the market or field; `setting the stage for`: led to; `reflects? broader`: name the trend, or cut; `valuable insights`: say what was learnt
- en-stiff-verbs (weak): Picks the stiff verb where a person uses the short one. Instead: Use the short verb. Terms: `utili[sz](?:e|es|ed|ing)`: use; `relocated`: moved; `authored`: wrote; `attempted`: tried
- en-vague-connection (weak): Joins two things with a loose word of connection and never says how. Instead: Give the actual relation: who did what, and where.
