# Signs of AI writing: English, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- en-stock-openers (usual): Uses a stock line about the modern world, or about unlocking potential. Instead: Start with the subject. Words: in today's modern age: cut; navigate the complexity: say what the task is; unlock the power: say what becomes possible
- en-ai-vocabulary (weak): Leans on the same few elevated words; one is chance, several together are not. Instead: Use the plain word, or the fact the word stands in for. Words: align with: matches; complexities: problems; crucial: say why it matters; deep dive: close look; delve: look at; foster: build; interconnectedness: links; interplay: how A affects B; intricate: detailed; let's dive in: cut; leverage: use; multifaceted: name the parts; nuanced: say what the nuance is; pivotal: say why it matters; robust: say how it holds up; seamless: smooth; tapestry: mix; transformative: say what changed; underscored, underscore a: shows; vibrant: lively
- en-claude-prompt-echo (weak; claude 3.5 and later): Opens by pointing back at the material it was given. Instead: Start with the answer; name the document where the reader needs it. Words: according to the text: cut; based on the text: cut; here's a summary: start with it
- en-copula-avoidance (weak; gpt 4 and later; gemini 1.5 and later): Avoids plain 'is' and 'has' with grander verbs. Instead: Write 'is' or 'has'. Words: boasts a: has; functions as a: is; holds the distinction of being: is; served as a: is; stands as a: is
- en-em-dash (weak; gpt 3.5 to 5.0; claude 3 and later): Uses spaced em dashes as pivots, more than a person writing the same kind of text. Instead: Use a comma, a colon, brackets or a full stop.
- en-even-sentences (weak): Writes sentence after sentence of one length and one shape. Instead: Let length follow the thought, some short and some long.
- en-gemini-phrases (weak; gemini 1.5 and later): Uses Gemini's breakdowns and its essay phrases. Instead: Say the thing directly. Words: complex interplay: how A affects B; extend far beyond: goes beyond; here's a breakdown: start with it; key improvements: what improved; manifest in various ways: give the ways; multi-pronged approach: name the steps; necessitates: needs
- en-gpt-2023-vocabulary (weak; gpt 3.5 to 4.x): Uses the words of 2023 to mid-2024 ChatGPT. Instead: Use the plain word. Words: boasts: has; bolster: support; enduring: lasting; garner: got; intricacy: details; meticulous: careful; testament: proof
- en-gpt-2024-vocabulary (weak; gpt 4 to 5.x): Uses the words of mid-2024 to mid-2026 ChatGPT. Instead: Use the plain word. Words: emphasising: stressing; enhance: improve; highlighting: showing; showcase: shows
- en-gpt-5-vocabulary (weak; gpt 5 to 5.x): Stresses media coverage and online presence, as GPT-5 does. Instead: Say what the source says, not what kind of source it is. Words: independent coverage: name the report; local media outlets: name them; has a strong online presence: cut; trade publications: name them
- en-gpt-6-vocabulary (weak; gpt 6 and later): Uses GPT-6's plainer words. Instead: Prefer the exact word. Words: dependable: reliable; echoed: say who repeated it; prioritise: put first; universally: say who
- en-grok-vocabulary (weak; grok 1 and later): Uses Grok's would-be scientific words, and its 'democratizes' and 'demystifies'. Instead: Use the plain word, or the measurement. Words: causal: say what caused what; correlate: track; democratise: opens up; demystify: explains; empirical: measured
- en-llama-phrases (weak; llama 4 and later): Uses Llama 4's business-essay phrases. Instead: Say what was done and what came of it. Words: far-reaching consequences: name them; gain a deeper understanding: learn; role in shaping: say what it changed; stay ahead of the competition: name the rival
- en-nominalisation (weak; gpt 4 to 4.x; llama 3 to 3.x): Packs sentences with nouns made from verbs. Instead: Turn the noun back into a verb, with a named subject.
- en-significance-phrases (weak): Reaches for set phrases that inflate a subject's importance. Instead: Say what happened and to whom. Words: enduring legacy: say what remains; evolving landscape: name the market or field; indelible mark: say what changed; is a testament to: shows; mark a pivotal shift: give the date and the change; play a vital role: say what it does; reflect broader: name the trend, or cut; setting the stage for: led to; valuable insights: say what was learnt
- en-stiff-verbs (weak): Picks the stiff verb where a person uses the short one. Instead: Use the short verb. Words: attempted: tried; authored: wrote; relocated: moved; utilise: use
- en-vague-connection (weak): Joins two things with a loose word of connection and never says how. Instead: Give the actual relation: who did what, and where.
