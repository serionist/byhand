# Signs of AI writing: Japanese, every model

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section; one phrase counts once.

- ja-bold-not-rendered (usual; email, message): Leaves Markdown bold against Japanese brackets, where it shows as bare asterisks. Instead: Drop the asterisks, or put them inside the brackets.
- ja-chat-residue (usual; post, document): Keeps a chat reply's framing in a document: answering the asker's question, offering the asker more; not an offer the writer makes to readers. Instead: Delete it; a document answers no one.
- ja-instruction-residue (usual; post, document): Leaves the writer's prompt in the text: the tone or style it was told to take; not a note on scope, readers or sources. Instead: Delete the line; keep what the reader gets.
- ja-ai-phrases (weak): Uses stock phrases that leave out who judged, what risk lurks, or who needs the thing. Instead: Name the person, the risk or the use the phrase stands in for. Terms: `(?:という|との)見立てです`: name who thinks so; `に潜む(?:リスク|課題|罠|危険)|(?:リスク|課題|罠|問題)が潜んで`: name the problem; `欠かせない存在と(?:なって|なりつつ)|今や[^。\n]{0,20}欠かせない存在`: say who uses it, for what; `が招く(?:混乱|失敗|事故)`: say what goes wrong; `という挙動を(?:排除|防止|阻止)`: say what stops what
- ja-calques (weak): Carries English idiom over word for word: the moment, on the table, project-level, a single source of truth. Instead: Say it the Japanese way: すぐに, 未定, この案件だけの. Terms: `テーブルに(?:載|残)|テーブルの上に(?:あ|残|載)`: 未定; `た瞬間に`: たらすぐ; `(?:プロジェクト|リポジトリ|ワークスペース|組織)レベルの`: say where it applies; `を正本(?:と|に)|の正本として`: 原本, or the file to check against
- ja-copy-fragments (weak): Writes prose like ad copy: clipped sentences stacked for effect, a noun after a lone comma, a list with no verb. Instead: Write the sentence out, with its verb.
- ja-em-dash (weak): Sets off asides or trails off with dashes in plain prose, an English habit; in essays and fiction a dash is a person's. Instead: Use 、 and a particle, or end the sentence.
- ja-false-agency (weak): Lets data, culture or problems act on their own: the data tells, a culture is fostered, issues surface. Instead: Name who did it or who noticed. Terms: `が物語って`: を見ると; `文化が醸成`: say who built it; `が浮き彫りに`: で分かった; `が浮かび上が`: で分かった
- ja-grand-kango (weak): Dresses a small personal experience in grand literary words. Instead: Tell it at its real size, in everyday words. Terms: `美学`: say what you like about it; `境地`: say where you got to; `極致`: the best; `宿命`: say why it was bound to happen; `虚飾`: show; `深淵`: depth, or cut; `禁欲的`: plain; `冷徹な`: cold; `真理(?:が|を|に|だ|で|へ|。)`: say what you found
- ja-hollow-closing (weak; post, document): Ends an article on a stock sign-off: how was it, do give it a try, start small. Instead: End on the last useful fact. Terms: `いかがでした`: cut; `ぜひ(?:参考にして|試して)みてください`: cut, or say who it suits; `まずは小さく始めましょう`: give the first step; `の重要性を再認識しました`: say what changed
- ja-inflated-endings (weak): Closes on a set tail that overstates or hedges a plain claim. Instead: End on the fact itself. Terms: `過言では`: cut, and state the fact; `に(?:他|ほか)なりません`: です; `と言えるでしょう`: でしょう
- ja-lesson-endings (weak): Ends each paragraph or small story on a lesson learnt. Instead: Let most end on what happened; draw a lesson once, if at all.
- ja-llm-function-words (weak): Leans, across a whole text, on function words chatbots use several times as often as people: いくつかの, という点, より before an adjective, に関する, かつ. Instead: Keep one where it carries meaning; give the number, or the plain verb.
- ja-noncommittal (weak): Takes no side: it depends, there are pros and cons, opinions differ. Instead: Say which you chose and why, or what it depends on. Terms: `メリットもあればデメリットも`: name the one that decides it; `一概には言えません`: say what it depends on; `賛否が分かれ`: say what you think; `ケースバイケース`: name the cases
- ja-pause-comma (weak): Puts a comma straight after a short topic, subject or object, where no reader needs a pause. Instead: Drop it; keep commas after long phrases, conditions and contrasts.
- ja-preface-filler (weak): Opens a sentence by clearing its throat: what matters is, to be honest, the conclusion first. Instead: Start with the point. Terms: `(?:^|。)(?:まず|ここで)?(?:重要|大事|大切)なのは、`: cut; `(?:^|。)結論から言うと`: cut; `(?:^|。)正直に言うと`: cut; `ここで注目すべきは`: cut; `見落とされがち`: say who misses it
- ja-stiff-wording (weak): Swaps a plain word for consultants' katakana, or a short verb for a stiff Sino-Japanese phrase that says the same. Instead: Use the plain Japanese word, or the short verb. Terms: `シナジー`: say what each side gains; `プライオリティ`: 優先順位; `アライン(?:メント)?`: すり合わせる; `アグリー`: 賛成です; `インテグリティ`: say what honesty means here; `シームレス`: say what no longer gets in the way; `を実現することができ`: できる; `することが可能(?:です|となり|になり)`: できます; `を活用することで`: を使えば; `多角的(?:な|に)`: name the angles; `包括的(?:な|に)`: say what it covers
- ja-stock-metaphors (weak): Explains with stock metaphors and metaphor verbs: a compass, a catalyst, crushing bugs, melting time. Instead: Say what the thing does, or what happened, in plain words. Terms: `起爆剤`: say what it starts; `羅針盤`: say how it guides; `両輪`: both are needed; `触媒(?:と|に|として)`: say what it speeds up; `勝ち筋`: a plan that can work; `(?:思考|仕事|人生|意思決定)の?OS`: way of thinking; `人生をハック`: 工夫する; `(?:一つ|1つ|ひとつ)ずつ潰し`: say what was fixed; `側に倒(?:す|し)`: say which you chose; `時間を溶かし`: say how long it took; `静かに(?:壊れ|失敗し)`: say what fails and that no one is told; `黙って(?:無視|捨て|破棄)`: say it is dropped with no notice; `地味に効(?:く|き|いて)`: say what it helps; `(?:ここ|そこ)で効いてくる`: say what changes
- ja-structure-announcements (weak; post, document): Announces the structure instead of giving it: there are three points, let's look at each in turn. Instead: Start on the first point; headings show the structure. Terms: `本記事では|本稿では`: cut; `(?:ポイント|理由|観点|コツ)は(?:3|３|三)つ(?:です|あります)`: give the points; `以下の(?:3|３|三)つの(?:観点|ポイント)`: give the points; `(?:順に)?見ていきましょう`: cut; `深掘りしていきます`: cut; `ここからが本題です`: cut
- ja-texture-words (weak): Uses vogue words that sound concrete or perceptive but say nothing anyone can check. Instead: Say the fact behind the word: who, what, how much. Terms: `肌感(?:覚)?`: what you saw, or the number; `解像度(?:が上が|を上げ|が高)`: understand in detail; `熱量`: say how much work went in; `腹落ち`: 納得; `温度感`: how keen they are; `手触り`: describe it; `泥臭(?:い|さ)`: name the hands-on work; `血の通った`: say what is personal in it; `地に足のついた`: practical; `等身大の`: ordinary
- ja-thin-human-markers (weak): Almost never uses ので, ながら or ば, and seldom quotes anyone. Instead: Use the connectives you would say aloud, and quote people in 「」.
- ja-trailing-colon (weak; post, document): Ends a sentence with a colon before a list, as English does. Instead: End it with 。
