# Signs of AI writing: Japanese, Claude

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section; one phrase counts once.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- ja-claude-version-calque (weak; claude 5.5 and later): Calls a version of a text 版, as English says version. Instead: Write 案 or バージョン, or name what changed. Terms: `(?:短い|長い|別の|今回の|簡単な|丁寧な)版(?:で|を|は|に|の|が|も|と)`: 案, or バージョン; `向けの版`: 向けの案
