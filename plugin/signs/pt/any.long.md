- pt-even-sentences (weak; post, document): Keeps sentences close to one length, where people mix short ones with long ones. Instead: Let a short sentence follow a long one where the thought needs it.
- pt-false-contrast (weak; post, document): The Portuguese form of a set-up contrast: não é apenas… é…, não é só… é…. Instead: Say what the thing is or does, with a figure or an example; drop the foil.

Instead: Cut the hinge; the sentence says the same without it. - pt-stacked-connectors (weak; post, document): Opens sentence after sentence with ademais, adicionalmente, além disso or outrossim; one of them is ordinary Portuguese. Instead: Join the facts with e or também, or let their order carry the link.
