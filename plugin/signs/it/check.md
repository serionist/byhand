# Signs of AI writing: Italian, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, two that one sign finds as well: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash however often it appears, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A pattern is what Grep runs on a file, case-insensitive and line by line; a sign with none is found by reading.

- it-dash-pivots (weak): Uses the long dash as a pivot: once in a sentence, before its punchline, or joined to the words on both sides. Italian sets an aside between two dashes and opens dialogue with one. Instead: Use a colon, a comma or a full stop; keep the dash for a real aside or dialogue. Pattern: `[^\W\d_]—[^\W\d_]|^(?:(?:[^—\n]|—[^—\n]*—)*[.!?]\s+)?[^—.!?\n]*[^—.!?:\n] — [^—.!?\n]+[.!?](?:\s*$|\s+[^—\s])`
- it-even-sentences (weak; post): Keeps full sentences close to one length and one shape, where people mix short ones with long ones. Instead: Let a short sentence follow a long one where the thought needs it.
- it-inflated-stakes (weak; post, document): Frames a plain fact as an epoch: in un mondo sempre più… Instead: Give the date, the number or the change itself. Words: in un mondo sempre più: cut Pattern: `\b(?:(?:in un mondo sempre più))\b`
- it-staccato-fragments (weak; post, document): Echoes a sentence with three or more short phrases with no verb, a full stop after each, for a punchy beat. A flyer's, a menu's or a job ad's list of facts, standing on its own, is not one. Instead: Say it in one sentence with a verb; one short phrase may stand alone for stress.
