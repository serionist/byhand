# Signs of AI writing: Turkish, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.
A pattern is what Grep runs on a file, case-insensitive and line by line; a sign with none is found by reading.

- tr-besides-this (weak; post, document): Adds one more point with Bunun yanı sıra at the start of a sentence, often more than once in a text. Instead: Join the point to the last sentence with ve, or start with the point itself. Words: bunun yanı sıra: ve, or cut Pattern: `\b(?:(?:bunun yan[ıI] s[ıI]ra))\b`
- tr-stock-idioms (weak; gpt 4 and later; document): Reaches for two idioms that GPT's Turkish writes far more than people do, rol oynamak and zemin hazırlamak, often several times in one text; one alone is ordinary Turkish. Instead: Say what the thing does or what it made possible: the verb itself, the number or the result. Words: rol oyn…: say what it does; zemin hazırla…: say what it made possible Pattern: `\b(?:(?:rol oyn\w*)|(?:zem[iİ]n haz[ıI]rl(?:a|[ıI]yor)\w*))\b`
