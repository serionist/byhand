# Signs of AI writing: English, every model

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more in one paragraph.

- en-chat-tool-markup (strong): Leaves a chat tool's file or document tags in the text. Instead: Delete the tag.
- en-corrective-framing (usual): Tells the reader how to take a source instead of saying what it shows. Instead: Say what the source shows; add a limit only where one is at issue.
- en-didactic-phrases (usual): Announces that something is worth noting before saying it. Instead: Say it. Terms: `it(?:['’]s| is) (?:important|crucial|critical|essential|worth) (?:to (?:note|remember|consider)|noting|remembering)`: cut
- en-significance-phrases (usual): Reaches for set phrases that inflate a subject's importance. Instead: Say what happened and to whom. Terms: `play(?:s|ed|ing)? an? (?:crucial|pivotal|vital) role`: say what it does; `(?:is|stands as|serves as) a testament to`: shows; `mark(?:s|ed|ing)? a (?:pivotal|significant) (?:moment|shift)`: give the date and the change; `enduring legacy`: say what remains; `indelible mark`: say what changed; `evolving landscape`: name the market or field; `setting the stage for`: led to; `reflects? broader`: name the trend, or cut; `valuable insights`: say what was learnt
- en-stock-openers (usual): Opens with a stock line about the modern world or diving in. Instead: Start with the subject. Terms: `in today['’]?s (?:fast-paced|digital|modern|ever-changing) (?:world|age|landscape|era)`: cut; `let['’]?s dive in(?:to)?`: cut; `deep dive`: close look; `navigat(?:e|es|ing) the complexit(?:y|ies)`: say what the task is; `unlock(?:s|ing)? the (?:power|potential)`: say what becomes possible
- en-trailing-participle (usual): Hangs an -ing clause of commentary off the end of a sentence. Instead: Stop at the full stop, or make the point its own sentence.
- en-ai-vocabulary (weak): Leans on the same few elevated words; one is chance, several together are not. Instead: Use the plain word, or the fact the word stands in for. Terms: `delv(?:e|es|ed|ing)`: look at; `pivotal`: name why it matters; `crucial`: needed; `multifaceted`: name the parts; `underscor(?:ed|ing)|underscores (?:the|a|an|its|how|that|this|their|our)`: shows; `foster(?:s|ed|ing)`: build; `interplay`: how A affects B; `interconnectedness`: links; `intricate`: detailed; `complexities`: problems; `nuanced`: say what the nuance is; `tapestry`: mix; `vibrant`: lively; `seamless(?:ly)?`: smooth; `robust`: say how it holds up; `transformative`: say what changed; `aligns? with`: matches; `leverag(?:e|es|ed|ing)`: use
- en-challenges-phrases (weak): Uses the stock wording of the challenges-and-prospects close. Instead: Name the problem. Terms: `despite (?:these|its|the|their) (?:challenges|limitations)`: name them; `faces? (?:several|many|numerous|a number of) challenges`: name them; `future (?:outlook|prospects)`: say what is planned
- en-copula-avoidance (weak): Avoids plain 'is' and 'has' with grander verbs. Instead: Write 'is' or 'has'. Terms: `serv(?:es|ed|ing) as an?`: is; `stands as an?`: is; `functions as an?`: is; `boasts an?`: has; `holds the distinction of being`: is
- en-even-sentences (weak): Keeps every sentence about the same length and shape. Instead: Let length follow the thought, some short and some long.
- en-negation-pivot (weak): Pivots on 'it's not X, it's Y' or 'not just X but Y'. Instead: Write the second half alone.
- en-promotional-phrases (weak): Uses the stock wording of a brochure. Instead: Say where it is and what it has. Terms: `nestled`: lies; `in the heart of`: in; `diverse array`: range; `rich (?:cultural )?heritage`: name it; `natural beauty`: say what is there; `groundbreaking`: new; `renowned`: known
- en-stiff-verbs (weak): Picks the stiff verb where a person uses the short one. Instead: Use the short verb. Terms: `utili[sz](?:e|es|ed|ing)`: use; `relocated`: moved; `authored`: wrote; `attempted`: tried
- en-stock-fiction-phrases (weak; post, document): Uses the stock beats and names of machine-written fiction. Instead: Write the thing this character does, in this place. Terms: `barely above a whisper`: quietly; `sun dipped (?:below|behind)`: the sun set; `shivers? (?:ran |run |running )?down (?:her|his|my|their) spine`: show the fear; `could(?:n['’]t| not) help but (?:feel|wonder)`: felt; `Elara`: another name
- en-vague-connection (weak): Says two things are 'associated with' or 'connected to' each other without saying how. Instead: State the relation: she ran it, he taught there.
