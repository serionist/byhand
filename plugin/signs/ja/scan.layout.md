Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A pattern, in backticks after a sign's brackets, is what Grep runs on a file, case-insensitive and line by line.

- any-colon-titles (weak; deck): `^#{1,6} [^：\n]+：`
- ja-count-titles (weak; deck): `^#{1,6} .*[2-9２-９二三四五六七八九]つの`
- ja-emoji-titles (weak; deck): `^#{1,6} (?:[0-9]+\. )?[\U0001F300-\U0001FAFF✅❌✨⚡⭐]`
