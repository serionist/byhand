# Signs of AI writing: Portuguese, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A pattern is what Grep runs on a file, case-insensitive and line by line; a sign with none is found by reading.

- pt-ai-vocabulary (weak; post, document): Leans on the words Portuguese model prose favours: enfatizando, vamos mergulhar. One alone is ordinary Portuguese. Instead: Use the plain word, or the fact the word stands in for. Words: enfatizando: cut, or say it plainly; vamos mergulhar: cut Pattern: `\b(?:(?:enfatizando)|(?:vamos mergulhar))\b`
- pt-closing-cheer (weak; post, document): Ends on a cheer that says nothing: o céu é o limite, o futuro é promissor, só o tempo dirá. Instead: End on the next step or the last fact. Words: o céu é o limite: cut; o futuro é promissor: cut; o melhor ainda está por vir: name what comes next; só o tempo dirá: cut Pattern: `\b(?:(?:o céu é o limite)|(?:o futuro é promissor)|(?:o melhor ainda está por vir)|(?:só o tempo dirá))\b`
- pt-dash-pivots (weak): Uses the long dash as a pivot: once in a sentence, before its punchline, or joined to the words on both sides. Portuguese sets an aside between two spaced travessões and opens dialogue with one. Instead: Use a colon, a comma or a full stop; keep the travessão for a real aside or dialogue. Pattern: `[^\W\d_]—[^\W\d_]|^(?:(?:[^—\n]|—[^—\n]*—)*[.!?]\s+)?[^—.!?\n]*[^—.!?:\n] — [^—.!?\n]+[.!?](?:\s*$|\s+[^—\s])`
- pt-even-sentences (weak; post, document): Keeps sentences close to one length, where people mix short ones with long ones. Instead: Let a short sentence follow a long one where the thought needs it.
- pt-inflated-stakes (weak; post, document): Frames a plain fact as an epoch: num mundo cada vez mais… Instead: Give the date, the number or the change itself. Words: num mundo cada vez mais: cut Pattern: `\b(?:(?:(?:em um|num) mundo cada vez mais))\b`
- pt-modifier-load (weak; post, document): Loads nouns with adjectives and intensifiers, in longer and rarer words than people use, with fewer verbs and pronouns. Instead: Keep the adjective that tells something; let a verb, a number or a name carry the rest.
- pt-stacked-connectors (weak; post, document): Opens sentence after sentence with ademais, adicionalmente, além disso or outrossim; one of them is ordinary Portuguese. Instead: Join the facts with e or também, or let their order carry the link.
- pt-staged-reveal (weak; post, document): Stages a point before making it: a question it answers at once (E a melhor parte?, O detalhe?), or já parou para pensar…? Instead: State the point; let the fact carry it. Pattern: `\b(?:e a melhor parte|o detalhe)\?\s+\S|\bjá (?:paraste|parou) para pensar\b`
