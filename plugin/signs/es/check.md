# Signs of AI writing: Spanish, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- es-ai-vocabulary (weak; post, document): Leans on the words Spanish model prose favours: adéntrate, de vanguardia, fascinante, sinergia, sumérgete, vibrante. One alone is ordinary Spanish. Instead: Use the plain word, or the fact the word stands in for. Words: adéntrate: cut; de vanguardia: say what is new; fascinante: say what is interesting; sinergia: say who does what together; sumérgete: cut; vibrante: lively, or the fact
- es-dash-pivots (weak): Uses the long dash the English way, as a pivot: spaced on both sides, or joined to the words on both sides. Spanish opens a raya before an aside and closes it after, and starts dialogue with one. Instead: Use a colon, a comma or a full stop; keep the raya for a real aside or dialogue.
- es-engagement-lines (weak; post): Ends a social post on stock bait: asks readers to comment below, or to save the post. Instead: Ask one real question, or end on the news. Words: déjame en comentarios: ask one real question; guarda este post: cut; te leo en comentarios: cut
- es-false-contrast (weak; post, document): The Spanish forms of a set-up contrast: no es solo… es…, no se trata de… se trata de…, or a run of Sin… fragments. Instead: Say what the thing does, with a figure or an example, and drop the foil.
- es-filler-hinges (weak; post, document): Hangs a sentence on a hinge that only announces it: cabe señalar que, es fundamental destacar que, vale la pena mencionar que. Instead: Cut the hinge; the sentence says the same without it. Words: cabe señalar que: cut; es importante señalar que: cut; sin lugar a duda: cut, or give the evidence; vale la pena señalar que: cut
- es-gpt-adjective-load (weak; gpt 3.5 to 4.x; post, document): Loads nouns with adjectives, more of them and more varied than people use, and uses fewer adverbs and pronouns. Instead: Keep the adjective that says something; let an adverb, a pronoun or a verb do the rest.
- es-gpt-short-sentences (weak; gpt 3.5 to 4.x; post, document): Chops Spanish prose into many short sentences, each ended with a full stop, where a person joins clauses with brackets, colons, commas or semicolons. Instead: Join what belongs together; let a long sentence carry an aside after a colon or in brackets.
- es-inflated-stakes (weak; post, document): Frames a plain fact as an epoch or a turning point: en el mundo actual, hoy más que nunca, punto de inflexión, un antes y un después. Instead: Give the date, the number or the change itself. Words: en el mundo actual: cut; en la era digital: cut; en un mundo cada vez más: cut; hoy más que nunca: cut; marca un antes y un después: say what changed; punto de inflexión: give the date; sentó las bases: say what it starts
- es-mixed-address (weak): Switches between tú and usted, or between imperative and infinitive steps, within one text. Instead: Keep one form of address for the reader and one form for the steps.
- es-service-script (weak; email, message): Stacks the customer-service script: a team working hard, an offer to get in touch, regret for the inconvenience, thanks for patience and understanding. Each alone is a person's set phrase. Instead: Name the cause and the date that now holds, then sign off once.
- es-staged-reveal (weak; post, document): Stages a point before making it: a question it answers at once (¿El resultado?), la buena noticia es que, la verdadera pregunta es. Instead: State the point; let the fact carry it.
