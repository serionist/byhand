PASS if the answer is a short letter in Hungarian to Kovács Péter, written with magázás (Ön, formal verb forms),
saying the heating has not worked for three days and asking for a repair person this week. A short note after the
letter about a detail it had to leave open (the sender's name, the flat number) is fine.
FAIL if it is not in Hungarian, uses tegezés, misses the request, opens with a line about the letter ("Íme a levél"),
or ends with a list of what it avoided or an offer to change it.
