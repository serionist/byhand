# Signs of AI writing: every language, GPT

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- any-chatgpt-citation-markup (strong; gpt 4 and later): Leaves ChatGPT's internal citation tokens in the text. Instead: Delete the token and cite the source, or cut the claim.
- any-chatgpt-search-token (strong; gpt 4 and later): Leaves ChatGPT's search placeholders, such as turn0search0, where a link stood. Instead: Delete the token and link the source, or cut the claim.
- any-knowledge-cutoff (strong; gpt 3.5 to 4.x): Warns that its knowledge stops at a training date. Instead: Check the current fact, or give the date of your source.
