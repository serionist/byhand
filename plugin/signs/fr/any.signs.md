# Signs of AI writing: French, every model

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- fr-stock-openers (usual): Starts with a sentence on how fast the world moves, before any fact about the subject. Instead: Cut the line; the first sentence is about the subject itself. Terms: `dans un monde en (?:constante|perpétuelle|pleine) (?:évolution|mutation)`: cut; `dans un monde qui (?:évolue|change|bouge) à (?:un rythme effréné|toute vitesse|grande vitesse)`: cut
- fr-ai-vocabulary (weak; post, document): Leans on the stock words models reach for: holistique, synergie, pierre angulaire, véritable before an abstract noun. One is chance; several are not. Instead: Use the plain word, or the fact the word stands for. Terms: `holistiques?`: say what is included; `synergies?`: say who does what together; `pierres? angulaires?`: base; `insuffl(?:er|e|ent|ant|é|ée|és|ées)`: say what changes; `façonn(?:er|e|ent|ant|é|ée|és|ées)`: say how; `niché(?:e|s|es)?`: give the place; `véritables? (?:atout|révolution|levier|pilier|moteur|tournant)s?`: drop véritable
- fr-article-rituals (weak; post, document): Talks about the article instead of writing it: announces its plan, invites the reader to dive in or explore together. Instead: Cut the tour-guide lines; the headings already show the plan. Terms: `dans (?:cet|ce) (?:article|billet|guide),? (?:nous allons|je vais|on va)`: cut; `(?:plongeons|explorons|découvrons)(?: ensemble| dans)`: cut; `sans plus (?:attendre|tarder)`: cut; `vous l['’]aurez compris`: cut; `entrons dans le vif du sujet`: cut
- fr-article-scaffold (weak; post, document): Builds a post or web article on a template: a bulleted summary up top, headings as questions or teasers (Ce que…), a closing FAQ, sources piled at the foot. Instead: Use headings that state their point, and cite each source where its fact is.
- fr-calques (weak; post, document): Carries an English idiom into French word for word: adresser un problème, délivrer de la valeur. Instead: Use the idiom French already has for it. Terms: `adress(?:er|e|ent|ons|ez|é|ée|ées) (?:un|le|ce|cet|les|des|ces) (?:problèmes?|enjeux?|défis?)`: traiter; `délivr(?:er|e|ent|ons|ez|ant) (?:de la |plus de )?valeur`: apporter; `navigu(?:er|e|ent|ons|ez|ant) (?:dans|à travers) (?:la|cette|les|ces) (?:complexités?|incertitudes?)`: say how
- fr-connector-openers (weak; post, document): Hangs each new paragraph on Par ailleurs, En outre, De plus or Cependant, so the link words do the work the facts should. One now and then is ordinary French. Instead: Let the order of the facts carry the link; keep a connector where the argument turns.
- fr-copula-avoidance (weak; post, document): Avoids a plain être or avoir with frames that place a thing in an approach or a logic: s'articule autour de, s'inscrit dans une démarche, se positionne comme. Instead: Use être or avoir, or an action verb with a person as its subject.
- fr-dash-asides (weak): Sets off asides between spaced em dashes, several to a paragraph. A dialogue dash, or a writer's single incise, is ordinary French. Instead: Fold the aside in with a comma, or give it its own sentence.
- fr-engagement-lines (weak; post): Frames a social post with stock lines: proud to announce, link in the comments, a closing Et vous ? or an opening Et si… ? Instead: Put the news and the link in the post itself, and end on the news.
- fr-english-headings (weak; post, document): Capitalises every word of a heading, as English titles do, or leaves an English label such as Key Takeaways in French text. Instead: Capitalise the first word and proper nouns only; title it in French.
- fr-false-contrast (weak; post, document): Frames a point against a smaller version of itself (not merely X, it is Y; not about X but Y), or tacks ', et non Y' onto a choice no reader was weighing. Correcting a real mistake (jeudi, et non vendredi) is not one. Instead: State the claim on its own; bring in the alternative only if a reader believes it.
- fr-filler-notice (weak; post, document): Flags the next point as worth noting, or as beyond proof, before getting to it. Instead: Delete the preface; the point stands without it. Terms: `il (?:convient|est (?:important|essentiel|crucial|intéressant)) de (?:noter|souligner)`: cut; `force est de constater`: cut; `n['’]est plus à (?:démontrer|prouver)`: give the proof; `il ressort de (?:cette|cet|ce) (?:analyse|étude|échange)`: give the finding
- fr-hashtag-block (weak; post): Ends a post on a block of four or more generic hashtags. Instead: End on the post's last sentence; tag only what a reader would search for.
- fr-hedge-stack (weak): Puts two hedges on one claim (pourrait potentiellement), where one already carries the doubt. Instead: Keep one hedge at the strength you mean; in a negotiation, leave the writer's own care alone.
- fr-inflated-stakes (weak; post, document): Lifts an ordinary event into an era or a turning point, or ends on a hopeful line about the future. Instead: Give the change in figures and names; stop at the last of them. Terms: `tournant (?:décisif|historique)`: say what changed; `moment charnière`: give the date; `(?:nouvelle|prochaine) ère`: say what starts; `à l['’]ère (?:du numérique|du digital|de l['’]ia|de l['’]intelligence artificielle)`: cut; `dans le paysage (?:actuel|numérique|digital)`: name the market; `aujourd['’]hui plus que jamais`: cut; `l['’]avenir s['’]annonce (?:prometteur|radieux)`: end on the fact; `(?:seul )?l['’]avenir nous le dira`: end on the fact; `plus (?:motivée?s?|déterminée?s?) que jamais`: say what comes next
- fr-performative-candour (weak; post, document): Promises honesty or straight talk before saying something quite ordinary. Instead: Drop the preface and let the statement stand. Terms: `soyons (?:honnêtes|francs|lucides)`: cut; `pour être (?:tout à fait )?(?:honnête|transparente?|franche?)`: cut; `sans langue de bois`: cut; `la vérité,? c['’]est que`: cut
- fr-slogan-mirrors (weak; post, document): Writes mirror slogans as in ad copy: a short Moins de… sentence answered by a Plus de… one, or two Ni… fragments closed on Juste… Instead: Say what changed and by how much: the hours saved, the price.
- fr-staged-reveal (weak; post, document): Teases a plain fact as a reveal, with a promised reason, a framed question or a twist, and keeps the reader waiting a sentence for it. Instead: Put the fact in the first sentence. Terms: `et voici (?:pourquoi|comment)`: give the reason; `mais ce n['’]est pas tout`: cut; `la réponse est simple`: give the answer; `le constat est sans appel`: give the figure; `la question n['’]est (?:plus|pas) de savoir si`: state the point; `c['’]est (?:là|ici) que (?:ça|cela|les choses) devien(?:t|nent) intéressant(?:es)?`: cut; `c['’]est (?:là|ici) qu(?:e |['’])[^.!?\n]{1,40} entre(?:nt)? en (?:jeu|scène)`: say what it does
