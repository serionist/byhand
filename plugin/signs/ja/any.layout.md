# Signs of AI writing: Japanese, every model, for decks and sheets

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- ja-chat-residue (usual; post, document, deck): Keeps a chat reply's framing in a document: answering the asker's question, offering the asker more; not an offer the writer makes to readers. Instead: Delete it; a document answers no one.
- ja-instruction-residue (usual; post, document, deck): Leaves the writer's prompt in the text: the tone or style it was told to take; not a note on scope, readers or sources. Instead: Delete the line; keep what the reader gets.
- ja-calques (weak; post, document, deck): Carries English idiom over word for word: the moment, on the table, project-level, a single source of truth. Instead: Say it the Japanese way: すぐに, 未定, この案件だけの. Words: た瞬間に: たらすぐ; を正本と, の正本として: 原本, or the file to check against; テーブルに残, テーブルの上にあ: 未定; 組織レベルの: say where it applies
- ja-count-titles (weak; deck): Titles a slide by how many items it holds, five steps or three reasons, where the title could state the point. Instead: Title the slide with what its items show; keep a number only when the number is the point: an amount, a share, a span of time.
- ja-emoji-titles (weak; deck): Opens slide titles with an emoji or a tick, as decoration. Instead: Let the title's words stand alone.
- ja-noncommittal (weak; post, document, deck): Takes no side: it depends, there are pros and cons, opinions differ. Instead: Say which you chose and why, or what it depends on. Words: ケースバイケース: name the cases; メリットもあればデメリットも: name the one that decides it; 一概には言えません: say what it depends on; 賛否が分かれ: say what you think
- ja-preface-filler (weak; post, document, deck): Opens a sentence by clearing its throat: what matters is, to be honest, the conclusion first. Instead: Start with the point. Words: 大事なのは、: cut; 結論から言うと: cut; 正直に言うと: cut; ここで注目すべきは: cut; 見落とされがち: say who misses it
- ja-stiff-wording (weak; post, document, deck): Swaps a plain word for consultants' katakana, or a short verb for a stiff Sino-Japanese phrase that says the same. Instead: Use the plain Japanese word, or the short verb. Words: することが可能です: できます; を実現することができ: できる; を活用することで: を使えば; アグリー: 賛成です; アライン: すり合わせる; インテグリティ: say what honesty means here; シナジー: say what each side gains; シームレス: say what no longer gets in the way; プライオリティ: 優先順位; 包括的な: say what it covers; 多角的な: name the angles
- ja-stock-metaphors (weak; post, document, deck): Explains with stock metaphors and metaphor verbs: a catalyst, a compass, crushing bugs, melting time. Instead: Name what it does, or what took place, in plain words. Words: 1つずつ潰し: say what was fixed; ここで効いてくる: say what changes; 両輪: both are needed; 人生OS: way of thinking; 人生をハック: 工夫する; 側に倒し: say which you chose; 勝ち筋: a plan that can work; 地味に効き: say what it helps; 時間を溶かし: say how long it took; 羅針盤: say how it guides; 触媒と: say what it speeds up; 起爆剤: say what it starts; 静かに壊れ: say what fails and that no one is told; 黙って捨て: say it is dropped with no notice
- ja-structure-announcements (weak; post, document, deck): Announces the structure instead of giving it: there are three points, let's look at each in turn. Instead: Start on the first point; headings show the structure. Words: ここからが本題です: cut; コツは3つです: give the points; 以下の3つの観点: give the points; 本記事では, 本稿では: cut; 深掘りしていきます: cut; 見ていきましょう: cut
- ja-texture-words (weak; post, document, deck): Uses vogue words that sound concrete or perceptive but say nothing anyone can check. Instead: Say the fact behind the word: how much, what, who. Words: 地に足のついた: practical; 手触り: describe it; 泥臭い: name the hands-on work; 温度感: how keen they are; 熱量: say how much work went in; 等身大の: ordinary; 肌感: what you saw, or the number; 腹落ち: 納得; 血の通った: say what is personal in it; 解像度が高: understand in detail
