# Signs of AI writing: English, Gemini

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- en-copula-avoidance (weak; gemini 1.5 and later): Avoids plain 'is' and 'has' with grander verbs. Instead: Write 'is' or 'has'. Terms: `boasts an?`: has; `holds the distinction of being`: is; `functions as an?`: is; `stands as an?`: is; `serv(?:es|ed|ing) as an?`: is
- en-gemini-phrases (weak; gemini 1.5 and later): Uses Gemini's breakdowns and its essay phrases. Instead: Say the thing directly. Terms: `here['’]s a breakdown`: start with it; `key improvements`: what improved; `extends? far beyond`: goes beyond; `multi-?pronged approach`: name the steps; `manifests? in various ways`: give the ways; `necessitates`: needs; `complex interplay`: how A affects B
