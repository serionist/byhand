# Signs of AI writing: French, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A pattern is what Grep runs on a file, case-insensitive and line by line; a sign with none is found by reading.

- fr-ai-vocabulary (weak; post, document): Uses the rare, grand words French model output is full of: holistique, pierre angulaire, synergie, véritable before an abstract noun. A single one proves nothing. Instead: Name what is actually there: the shared planning, the street, the two teams. Words: façonne: say how; holistique: say what is included; insuffle: say what changes; niché: give the place; pierre angulaire: base; synergie: say who does what together; véritable atout: drop véritable Pattern: `\b(?:(?:façonn(?:ant|e|é|ée|ées|ent|er|és))|(?:holistiques?)|(?:insuffl(?:ant|e|é|ée|ées|ent|er|és))|(?:niché(?:e|es|s)?)|(?:pierres? angulaires?)|(?:synergies?)|(?:véritables? (?:atout|levier|moteur|pilier|révolution|tournant)s?))\b`
- fr-article-rituals (weak; post, document): Talks about the article instead of writing it: announces its plan, invites the reader to dive in or explore together. Instead: Cut the tour-guide lines; the headings already show the plan. Words: dans ce guide, on va: cut; explorons dans: cut; entrons dans le vif du sujet: cut; sans plus tarder: cut; vous l'aurez compris: cut Pattern: `\b(?:(?:dans (?:ce|cet) (?:article|billet|guide),? (?:je vais|nous allons|on va))|(?:(?:découvrons|explorons|plongeons) (?:dans|ensemble))|(?:entrons dans le vif du sujet)|(?:sans plus (?:attendre|tarder))|(?:vous l['’]aurez compris))\b`
- fr-article-scaffold (weak; post, document): Lays a post out like a template web page: closing FAQ, question headings, source list at the foot, summary bullets up top, teaser headings (Ce que…). Instead: Title sections by what they say, and attach each source to the claim it backs.
- fr-calques (weak; post, document): Uses business English turned into French verbs: adresser un enjeu, naviguer dans l'incertitude. Instead: Use the idiom French already has for it. Words: adresse l'défi: traiter; délivre valeur: apporter; navigue dans l'complexité: say how Pattern: `\b(?:(?:adress(?:e|é|ée|ées|ent|er|ez|ons) (?:ce |ces |cet |des |l['’]|le |les |un )(?:défis?|enjeux?))|(?:délivr(?:ant|e|ent|er|ez|ons) (?:de la |plus de )?valeur)|(?:navigu(?:ant|e|ent|er|ez|ons) (?:à travers|dans) (?:ces |cette |l['’]|la |les )(?:complexités?|incertitudes?)))\b`
- fr-connector-openers (weak; post, document): Opens most paragraphs on a formal link word, so the text reads as a pile of additions. One now and then is ordinary French. Instead: Let the order of the facts carry the link; keep a connector where the argument turns.
- fr-copula-avoidance (weak; post, document): Avoids a plain être or avoir with reflexive frames that set a thing around axes, in a role or inside an approach. Instead: Say what the thing is or does, and who does it.
- fr-dash-asides (weak): Encloses aside after aside in spaced em dashes, several to a paragraph; an occasional incise and dialogue dashes are French typography, not this. Instead: Rewrite the sentence so the aside isn't needed, or move it to the next one.
- fr-engagement-lines (weak; post): Frames a social post with stock lines: closing on Et vous ?, linking in the comments, opening on Et si… ?, proud announcements. Instead: Open with the news, paste the link where it is mentioned, and stop once the news is told. Pattern: `^\W*et si\b[^\n]*\?\s*$|^\W*et (?:toi|vous)(?:,[^\n]*\?|\s?\?)\s*$|\b(?:je suis|nous sommes) (?:tellement |très )?(?:fi(?:er|ère|ères|ers)|heureuses?|heureux|ravie?s?) d['’](?:accueillir|annoncer)|\b(?:la suite|les détails|lien) en (?:premier )?commentaire`
- fr-english-headings (weak; post, document): A French heading that looks translated: capitals all along the line, or English section labels such as Wrap-Up. Instead: Retype it as a French memo would set it: Notre bilan du premier trimestre.
- fr-false-contrast (weak; post, document): The French forms of a set-up contrast whose first half is played down with juste, seulement or simple. Correcting a real mistake, or scoping a plan, is not one. Instead: Give Y its evidence, an example or a figure, instead of a foil. Pattern: `n['’]est pas (?:juste |qu['’]|seulement |simplement |un simple |une simple )[^.!?;:\n]{1,60}[,:]\s*c['’]est|il ne s['’]agit pas (?:juste |seulement |simplement )(?:d['’]|de )[^.!?;\n]{1,60},\s*mais (?:d['’]|de )`
- fr-filler-notice (weak; post, document): The French form of a didactic aside: an impersonal il convient or il est important clause in front of the point itself. Instead: Begin the sentence where the clause after que begins. Words: il convient de noter qu': cut Pattern: `\b(?:(?:il (?:convient|est (?:essentiel|important|intéressant)) de (?:noter|souligner) qu(?:['’]|e)))\b`
- fr-hashtag-block (weak; post): Closes a post with a run of broad tags that name a mood or a sector, not anything a reader would look up. Instead: Let the post end on its last sentence. Pattern: `(?:^|\s)#[^\W\d_]\w*(?:[ \t]+#[^\W\d_]\w*){3,}`
- fr-hedge-stack (weak): Softens one claim twice over (pourrait potentiellement), so the reader can't tell how likely it is. Instead: Pick the verb that matches the odds (peut, risque de, va); in a negotiation, leave the writer's own care alone. Pattern: `(?:éventuellement|possiblement|potentiellement) susceptibles? d|pourrai(?:ent|t) éventuellement peut-être|pourrai(?:ent|t) potentiellement`
- fr-inflated-stakes (weak; post, document): French stock phrases of a bright future, eras and turning points. Instead: Write it as a status update would: what happened, and on which date. Words: à l'ère de l'ia: cut; aujourd'hui plus que jamais: cut; dans le paysage actuel: name the market; l'avenir nous le dira: end on the fact; l'avenir s'annonce radieux: end on the fact; moment charnière: give the date; nouvelle ère: say what starts; plus motivé que jamais: say what comes next; tournant décisif: say what changed Pattern: `\b(?:(?:à l['’]ère de l['’](?:ia|intelligence artificielle))|(?:aujourd['’]hui plus que jamais)|(?:dans le paysage (?:actuel|digital|numérique))|(?:l['’]avenir nous le dira)|(?:l['’]avenir s['’]annonce (?:prometteur|radieux))|(?:moments? charnières?)|(?:(?:nouvelle|prochaine) ère)|(?:plus (?:déterminée?s?|motivée?s?) que jamais)|(?:tournants? (?:décisifs?|historiques?)))\b`
- fr-performative-candour (weak; post, document): Promises honesty or straight talk before saying something quite ordinary. Instead: Start at the statement; if it is candid, the reader will see it. Words: la vérité, c'est que: cut; pour être franc: cut; sans langue de bois: cut; soyons francs: cut Pattern: `\b(?:(?:la vérité,? c['’]est que)|(?:pour être (?:tout à fait )?(?:franc(?:he)?s?|honnêtes?|transparente?s?))|(?:sans langue de bois)|(?:soyons (?:franc(?:he)?s|honnêtes?|lucides)))\b`
- fr-slogan-mirrors (weak; post, document): Writes ad-copy fragments in matched pairs: a short Moins de… sentence and then a Plus de… one, or two Ni… and then Juste… Instead: Write one plain sentence with the real numbers in it. Pattern: `(?:^|[.!?]\s+)moins de [^.!?\n]{1,40}\.\s+plus de [^.!?\n]{1,40}[.!]|(?:^|[.!?]\s+)ni [^.!?\n]{1,40}\.\s*ni [^.!?\n]{1,40}\.\s*(?:juste|seulement|simplement)\b`
- fr-staged-reveal (weak; post, document): Builds a small drumroll in front of an ordinary fact: a reason promised for the next sentence, a twist still to come. Instead: Cut the teaser; the sentence after it already holds the point. Words: c'est là qu'entre en jeu: say what it does; c'est là que ça devient intéressant: cut; et voici comment: give the reason; la question n'est pas de savoir si: state the point; mais ce n'est pas tout: cut Pattern: `\b(?:(?:c['’]est (?:ici|là) qu(?:['’]|e [^.!?\n]{1,40} )entre(?:nt)? en (?:jeu|scène))|(?:c['’]est (?:ici|là) que (?:ça|cela|les choses) devien(?:nent|t) intéressant(?:es)?)|(?:et voici (?:comment|pourquoi))|(?:la question n['’]est (?:pas|plus) de savoir si)|(?:mais ce n['’]est pas tout))\b`
- fr-stock-openers (weak; post, document): Opens a text or a paragraph on how fast the world moves, before any fact about the subject. Instead: Begin with the news itself. Pattern: `^\W*dans un monde (?:en (?:constante|perpétuelle|pleine) (?:évolution|mutation)|qui (?:bouge|change|évolue) à (?:grande vitesse|toute vitesse|un rythme effréné))\b`
