# Signs of AI writing: Turkish, GPT, for posts and documents

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- tr-stock-idioms (weak; gpt 4 and later; document): Reaches for two idioms that GPT's Turkish writes far more than people do, rol oynamak and zemin hazırlamak, often several times in one text; one alone is ordinary Turkish. Instead: Say what the thing does or what it made possible: the verb itself, the number or the result. Words: rol oyn…: say what it does; zemin hazırla…: say what it made possible
