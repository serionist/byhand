# Signs of AI writing: German, every model

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- de-dive-in (weak; post, document): Invites the reader to dive in with it, or offers to break the subject down, as a chatbot's walk-through does. Instead: Start with the first point. Terms: `lass(?:en sie|t)? uns (?:\w+ ){0,3}eintauchen`: cut; `lass(?:en sie)? mich (?:das|dies|es) (?:\w+ )?aufschlüsseln`: give the parts; `tauch(?:en|t) (?:ihr|sie|wir) (?:\w+ ){0,3}ein`: cut
- de-em-dash (weak): Sets off a clause with the long English dash (—); the spaced short dash (–) is German's own, and a person's. Instead: Use a comma or a full stop, or the spaced short dash.
- de-emphasis-phrases (weak; post, document): Tells the reader that a point deserves attention before making it. Instead: Make the point; the reader decides what matters. Terms: `(?:dabei ist es|es ist|hierbei ist es) (?:auch |dabei |jedoch )?(?:entscheidend|wichtig),? zu (?:bemerken|betonen)`: cut, and give the fact
- de-future-closers (weak; post, document): Closes on a stock look ahead that promises nothing anyone can check. Instead: End on the last fact, or the next date. Terms: `(?:darf man|man darf|wir dürfen) gespannt sein`: say what is decided, and when; `(?:die|unsere) zukunft (?:gemeinsam )?(?:zu )?gestalten|gestalten wir (?:gemeinsam )?(?:die|unsere) zukunft`: say what you will do next; `die zukunft sieht (?:\w+ )?(?:glänzend|rosig|vielversprechend) aus`: give the plan or the forecast; `nur die zeit wird (?:es )?zeigen`: cut; `spannende zeiten (?:liegen|stehen)`: cut
- de-hedging (weak; post, document): Softens plain claims with kann and könnte throughout, or stacks two hedges on one claim. Instead: State it, or name the one condition that makes it uncertain.
- de-list-announcers (weak; post, document): Announces a list with a stock phrase of plenty before naming the items; common in people's ad copy too. Instead: Name the items, or give the number. Terms: `eine[rn]? vielzahl an`: viele, or the number; `eine[rn]? breiten? palette (?:an|von)`: name the items
- de-participle-asides (weak): Hangs a present participle on a sentence to say what the fact shows, a form German prose seldom uses. Instead: Stop at the fact; if its meaning matters, give it a sentence of its own. Terms: `verdeutlichend|widerspiegelnd|gewährleistend|unterstreichend|symbolisierend|hervorhebend|betonend`: cut
- de-promo-words (weak; post, document): Sells a place, a firm or a product with brochure words where a neutral one would do. Instead: Describe what is there, and let the reader judge. Terms: `atemberaubend\w*`: say what one sees; `bahnbrechend\w*`: say what is new, and since when; `im herzen (?:der|des|von)`: in, or the address; `kulturelle[nrs]? teppich\w*`: name the groups; `paradigmenwechsel\w*`: say what changed for whom; `reiche[nrs]? (?:kulturelle[nrs]? )?(?:erbes?|geschichte|traditionen?)`: name one piece of it; `revolutionier\w*`: say what it changes; `wegweisend\w*`: say who followed
- de-rhetorical-openers (weak; post, document): Opens with a question nobody asked: what if, have you ever wondered. Instead: Start with the point or the news.
- de-significance-phrases (weak; post, document): Dresses a plain subject in set phrases of legacy and deep meaning. Instead: Give the fact or the number that shows it matters. Terms: `bleibende[ns]? (?:erbes?|vermächtnis(?:ses)?)`: say what remains; `(?:dienen|dient|diente|dienten|stand|standen|stehen|steht) als zeugnis`: zeigt; `fasziniert (?:bis heute|nach wie vor|weiterhin)`: say who still comes or reads it; `schlüsselmoment\w*`: give the date and what changed; `tief ?verwurzelt\w*`: say since when, or cut; `unerschütterlich\w*`: say what they kept doing; `unterstr(?:eichen|eicht|ich|ichen) (?:deren|dessen|die|ihre|seine) (?:\w+ )?(?:bedeutung|relevanz|rolle)`: zeigt, or the fact itself
- de-sowohl-triad (weak; post, document): Stretches sowohl … als auch over three items: a rule of three in German dress. Instead: List the items with und, or keep the two that matter.
- de-stiff-connectives (weak): Starts sentence after sentence with ferner, des Weiteren or darüber hinaus; one of them, or a paper full of them, is a person's. Instead: Use und or auch, or let the order of the sentences carry the link.
- de-stock-openers (weak; post, document): Opens on a stock line about fast-changing modern times. Instead: Start with the subject. Terms: `im (?:digitalen )?zeitalter (?:der|von) (?:digitalisierung|ki|künstlicher intelligenz)`: cut; `in (?:der|unserer) heutigen,? (?:digitalen|modernen|schnelllebigen|vernetzten) (?:gesellschaft|welt|zeit)`: cut, or name the year; `in einer sich (?:\w+ ){0,2}(?:verändernden|wandelnden) welt`: cut
- de-title-case-headings (weak): Capitalises every word of a heading the English way, articles, und, für and adjectives included. Instead: Capitalise the first word, nouns and names only.
