# Signs of AI writing: Russian, every model

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- ru-title-case-headings (usual): Capitalises every word of a heading, an English habit; Russian capitalises the first word and names only. Headings set in capitals throughout, as contracts use, are not this. Instead: Capitalise the first word and proper names; leave и, в, на and the rest small.
- ru-calques (weak; post, document): Pairs a Russian verb and noun the way English pairs them, not the way Russian does. Instead: Use the Russian pairing: решить проблему, успеть к сроку. Terms: `адрес(?:овать|ует|уют|овал\w*|уем) (?:эт\w+ |наш\w+ |ваш\w+ |главн\w+ )?(?:бол|задач|проблем)\w*`: решить; `встрет\w+ дедлайн\w*`: успеть к сроку; `достав(?:лять|ляет|ляют|ляем|лял\w*|ляя|ить|ит|ят|им|ил\w*) ценность`: say what the user gets
- ru-challenges-formula (weak): Ends on a stock pair: difficulties named in general, then a bright future. Instead: Name the problem and what is being done about it, or stop. Terms: `будущее выглядит (?:ярким|светлым|многообещающ\w*)`: give the next step; `несмотря на (?:все|существующие|эти) (?:вызовы|трудности)`: cut; `продолжа\w+ процветать`: give a number; `(?:сталкива|столкну)\w+ с (?:целым )?рядом вызов\w*`: name the problem
- ru-chat-residue (weak; post, document): Leaves a chat assistant's sign-off in a post or a document: a hope that it helped, an offer of more. Instead: Delete it; end on the last useful fact. Terms: `буду рад\w* помочь`: cut; `если (?:вы )?хотите (?:спросить|узнать) (?:что-нибудь|что-то) ещ[её]`: cut; `надеюсь, (?:мой ответ|эта информация|это) (?:был полезен|была полезна|было полезно|помог|помогла|помогло|поможет)`: cut
- ru-chopped-fragments (weak; post, document): Breaks a point into a run of verbless scraps for effect. Instead: Write one full sentence with its verb.
- ru-dangling-participle (weak): Starts on a деепричастие whose doer never turns up as the subject. Instead: Name the doer as the subject.
- ru-didactic-phrases (weak): Tells the reader what is worth noting before saying it. Instead: Say it; the reader decides what matters. Terms: `(?:крайне|критически) важно (?:отметить|подч[её]ркнуть)`: cut; `стоит обратить внимание на то`: cut
- ru-math-signs (weak; post, document): Puts =, >, < or vs between words in running prose. Instead: Write the relation out in words.
- ru-office-register (weak; post, document): Writes a post or an article in office register: осуществлять, является, данный, в целях, strings of genitives. Letters, minutes, contracts and reports use it by custom; leave them. Instead: Use the plain verb and этот, and say who does what.
- ru-paragraph-stack (weak; post, document): Links paragraphs only with кроме того or также, so none grows out of the one before. Instead: Let each paragraph answer, extend or turn on the one before it.
- ru-poster-maxim (weak; post, document): Puts a claim as a two-noun maxim; the dash in it is plain Russian, the maxim is the sign. Instead: Say what actually happened, with a number if there is one.
- ru-promotional-words (weak; post, document): Describes a place or a business in tourist-brochure words. Instead: Say where it is and what is there. Terms: `(?:[а-яё]{3,}|[а-яё]|[а-мо-яё][а-яё]|н[а-дж-яё]) мо(?:гут|жет) похвастаться`: есть; `богат\w* (?:историческ|культурн)\w+ (?:наследи|прошл)\w*`: name what is there; `в самом сердце`: в центре, or the address; `захватывающ\w* дух`: describe it; `потрясающ\w+ (?:природн\w+ )?красот\w*`: describe it
- ru-significance-phrases (weak): Hangs symbolism and turning points on ordinary facts with set phrases. Instead: Say what happened; name the change only if you can. Terms: `(?:выступа|служ|явля)\w* (?:живым |ярким )?(?:напоминанием|символом)`: say what it shows; `знамен(?:ова|у)\w*`: начинается, or give the date; `неизгладим\w+ след\w*`: say what changed; `непоколебим\w*`: cut; `поворотн\w+ (?:момент|пункт|этап)\w*`: give the date and the change; `символизир\w*`: say who reads it so
- ru-stock-openers (weak): Opens with a line about the modern world or what everyone knows. Instead: Start with the subject. Terms: `в современном мире`: cut; `каждый из нас`: say who; `(?:ни для кого )?не секрет, что`: cut
- ru-tacked-on-participles (weak): Ends a sentence on a participle clause that draws a moral from the fact. Instead: Stop at the fact, or give the cause in a sentence of its own. Terms: `демонстрируя`: cut; `отражая`: name the cause; `подч[её]ркивая`: cut, or say who stresses it; `способствуя`: say what it changed
- ru-therapy-register (weak; post, document): Comforts the reader with stock lines of emotional support. Instead: Give the fact that would reassure, or cut. Terms: `вы не одиноки`: say who else has it; `давайте признаем`: cut; `и это (?:нормально|окей)`: cut; `позвольте себе`: cut
- ru-throat-clearing (weak; post, document): Spends a sentence on what it is about to say, or on how frank it is being. Instead: Start with the point. Terms: `без лишних слов`: cut; `вот в ч[её]м (?:фокус|штука)`: cut; `вот что (?:важно|нужно) знать`: cut; `давайте начистоту`: cut; `давайте разбер[её]мся`: cut; `если копнуть глубже`: cut; `настоящий вопрос в том`: ask it; `погрузимся в`: cut
- ru-vague-authority (weak): Leans on unnamed experts and analysts. Instead: Name the person or the study, or drop the claim. Terms: `исследователи утверждают`: name them; `некоторые критики`: name them
- ru-verb-echo (weak): Reuses the last sentence's verb for the next sentence's new subject. Instead: Give each subject the verb that fits it; repeat one only for emphasis.
