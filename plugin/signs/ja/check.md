# Signs of AI writing: Japanese, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.
A pattern is what Grep runs on a file, case-insensitive and line by line; a sign with none is found by reading.

- ja-bold-not-rendered (usual; email, message): Leaves Markdown bold against Japanese brackets, where it shows as bare asterisks. Instead: Drop the asterisks, or put them inside the brackets. Pattern: `[」』）]\*\*[ぁ-んァ-ヶー一-龠々]|[ぁ-んァ-ヶー一-龠々]\*\*[「『（]`
- ja-chat-residue (usual; post, document): Keeps a chat reply's framing in a document: answering the asker's question, offering the asker more; not an offer the writer makes to readers. Instead: Delete it; a document answers no one.
- ja-instruction-residue (usual; post, document): Leaves the writer's prompt in the text: the tone or style it was told to take; not a note on scope, readers or sources. Instead: Delete the line; keep what the reader gets.
- ja-ai-phrases (weak): Uses stock phrases that leave out who judged, what risk lurks, or who needs the thing. Instead: Name the person, the risk or the use the phrase stands in for. Words: が招く事故: say what goes wrong; との見立てです: name who thinks so; という挙動を排除: say what stops what; に潜む罠, 罠が潜んで: name the problem; 欠かせない存在となって, 今や…欠かせない存在: say who uses it, for what Pattern: `(?:が招く(?:事故|失敗|混乱))|(?:(?:という|との)見立てです)|(?:という挙動を(?:排除|防止|阻止))|(?:に潜む(?:リスク|危険|罠|課題)|(?:リスク|問題|罠|課題)が潜んで)|(?:欠かせない存在と(?:なって|なりつつ)|今や[^。\n]{0,20}欠かせない存在)`
- ja-calques (weak; post, document): Carries English idiom over word for word: the moment, on the table, project-level, a single source of truth. Instead: Say it the Japanese way: すぐに, 未定, この案件だけの. Words: た瞬間に: たらすぐ; を正本と, の正本として: 原本, or the file to check against; テーブルに残, テーブルの上にあ: 未定; 組織レベルの: say where it applies Pattern: `(?:た瞬間に)|(?:を正本(?:と|に)|の正本として)|(?:テーブルに(?:残|載)|テーブルの上に(?:あ|残|載))|(?:(?:プロジェクト|リポジトリ|ワークスペース|組織)レベルの)`
- ja-claude-version-calque (weak; claude 5.5 and later): Calls a version of a text 版, as English says version. Instead: Write 案 or バージョン, or name what changed. Words: 別の版が: 案, or バージョン; 向けの版: 向けの案 Pattern: `(?:(?:丁寧な|今回の|別の|短い|簡単な|長い)版(?:が|で|と|に|の|は|も|を))|(?:向けの版)`
- ja-copy-fragments (weak; post, document): Writes prose like ad copy: clipped sentences stacked for effect, a noun after a lone comma, a list with no verb. Instead: Write the sentence out, with its verb. Pattern: `(?:^|[。！？])(?:[^。！？、\s「」]{1,5}[。！]){3}`
- ja-em-dash (weak): Sets off asides or trails off with dashes in plain prose, an English habit; in essays and fiction a dash is a person's. Instead: Use 、 and a particle, or end the sentence. Pattern: `[ぁ-んァ-ヶー一-龠々」』）、]\s?(?:――|——|─{1,2}|―|—)[^―—─\n]{1,40}(?:――|——|─{1,2}|―|—)\s?[ぁ-んァ-ヶー一-龠々「『（。、]|[ぁ-んァ-ヶー一-龠々」』）、]\s?(?:――|——|─{1,2}|―|—)\s?[ぁ-んァ-ヶー一-龠々「『（。]`
- ja-false-agency (weak): Lets data, culture or problems act on their own: the data tells, a culture is fostered, issues surface. Instead: Name who did it or who noticed. Words: が浮かび上が: で分かった; が浮き彫りに: で分かった; が物語って: を見ると; 文化が醸成: say who built it Pattern: `(?:が浮かび上が)|(?:が浮き彫りに)|(?:が物語って)|(?:文化が醸成)`
- ja-grand-kango (weak): Dresses a small personal experience in grand literary words. Instead: Tell it at its real size, in everyday words. Words: 冷徹な: cold; 境地: say where you got to; 宿命: say why it was bound to happen; 極致: the best; 深淵: depth, or cut; 真理。: say what you found; 禁欲的: plain; 美学: say what you like about it; 虚飾: show Pattern: `(?:冷徹な)|(?:境地)|(?:宿命)|(?:極致)|(?:深淵)|(?:真理(?:。|が|だ|で|に|へ|を))|(?:禁欲的)|(?:美学)|(?:虚飾)`
- ja-hollow-closing (weak; post, document): Ends an article on a stock sign-off: how was it, do give it a try, start small. Instead: End on the last useful fact. Words: いかがでした: cut; ぜひ試してみてください: cut, or say who it suits; の重要性を再認識しました: say what changed; まずは小さく始めましょう: give the first step Pattern: `(?:いかがでした)|(?:ぜひ(?:参考にして|試して)みてください)|(?:の重要性を再認識しました)|(?:まずは小さく始めましょう)`
- ja-inflated-endings (weak): Closes on a set tail that overstates or hedges a plain claim. Instead: End on the fact itself. Words: と言えるでしょう: でしょう; に他なりません: です; 過言では: cut, and state the fact Pattern: `(?:と言えるでしょう)|(?:に(?:ほか|他)なりません)|(?:過言では)`
- ja-lesson-endings (weak): Ends each paragraph or small story on a lesson learnt. Instead: Let most end on what happened; draw a lesson once, if at all.
- ja-llm-function-words (weak): Leans, across a whole text, on function words chatbots use several times as often as people: いくつかの, という点, より before an adjective, に関する, かつ. Instead: Keep one where it carries meaning; give the number, or the plain verb.
- ja-noncommittal (weak; post, document): Takes no side: it depends, there are pros and cons, opinions differ. Instead: Say which you chose and why, or what it depends on. Words: ケースバイケース: name the cases; メリットもあればデメリットも: name the one that decides it; 一概には言えません: say what it depends on; 賛否が分かれ: say what you think Pattern: `(?:ケースバイケース)|(?:メリットもあればデメリットも)|(?:一概には言えません)|(?:賛否が分かれ)`
- ja-pause-comma (weak): Puts a comma straight after a short topic, subject or object, where no reader needs a pause. Instead: Drop it; keep commas after long phrases, conditions and contrasts.
- ja-preface-filler (weak; post, document): Opens a sentence by clearing its throat: what matters is, to be honest, the conclusion first. Instead: Start with the point. Words: 大事なのは、: cut; 結論から言うと: cut; 正直に言うと: cut; ここで注目すべきは: cut; 見落とされがち: say who misses it Pattern: `(?:(?:^|。)(?:ここで|まず)?(?:大事|大切|重要)なのは、)|(?:(?:^|。)結論から言うと)|(?:(?:^|。)正直に言うと)|(?:ここで注目すべきは)|(?:見落とされがち)`
- ja-stiff-wording (weak; post, document): Swaps a plain word for consultants' katakana, or a short verb for a stiff Sino-Japanese phrase that says the same. Instead: Use the plain Japanese word, or the short verb. Words: することが可能です: できます; を実現することができ: できる; を活用することで: を使えば; アグリー: 賛成です; アライン: すり合わせる; インテグリティ: say what honesty means here; シナジー: say what each side gains; シームレス: say what no longer gets in the way; プライオリティ: 優先順位; 包括的な: say what it covers; 多角的な: name the angles Pattern: `(?:することが可能(?:です|となり|になり))|(?:を実現することができ)|(?:を活用することで)|(?:アグリー)|(?:アライン(?:メント)?)|(?:インテグリティ)|(?:シナジー)|(?:シームレス)|(?:プライオリティ)|(?:包括的(?:な|に))|(?:多角的(?:な|に))`
- ja-stock-metaphors (weak; post, document): Explains with stock metaphors and metaphor verbs: a compass, a catalyst, crushing bugs, melting time. Instead: Name what it does, or what took place, in plain words. Words: 1つずつ潰し: say what was fixed; ここで効いてくる: say what changes; 両輪: both are needed; 人生OS: way of thinking; 人生をハック: 工夫する; 側に倒し: say which you chose; 勝ち筋: a plan that can work; 地味に効き: say what it helps; 時間を溶かし: say how long it took; 羅針盤: say how it guides; 触媒と: say what it speeds up; 起爆剤: say what it starts; 静かに壊れ: say what fails and that no one is told; 黙って捨て: say it is dropped with no notice Pattern: `(?:(?:1つ|ひとつ|一つ)ずつ潰し)|(?:(?:ここ|そこ)で効いてくる)|(?:両輪)|(?:(?:人生|仕事|思考|意思決定)の?OS)|(?:人生をハック)|(?:側に倒(?:し|す))|(?:勝ち筋)|(?:地味に効(?:いて|き|く))|(?:時間を溶かし)|(?:羅針盤)|(?:触媒(?:と|として|に))|(?:起爆剤)|(?:静かに(?:壊れ|失敗し))|(?:黙って(?:捨て|無視|破棄))`
- ja-structure-announcements (weak; post, document): Announces the structure instead of giving it: there are three points, let's look at each in turn. Instead: Start on the first point; headings show the structure. Words: ここからが本題です: cut; コツは3つです: give the points; 以下の3つの観点: give the points; 本記事では, 本稿では: cut; 深掘りしていきます: cut; 見ていきましょう: cut Pattern: `(?:ここからが本題です)|(?:(?:コツ|ポイント|理由|観点)は(?:3|三|３)つ(?:あります|です))|(?:以下の(?:3|三|３)つの(?:ポイント|観点))|(?:本記事では|本稿では)|(?:深掘りしていきます)|(?:(?:順に)?見ていきましょう)`
- ja-texture-words (weak; post, document): Uses vogue words that sound concrete or perceptive but say nothing anyone can check. Instead: Say the fact behind the word: who, what, how much. Words: 地に足のついた: practical; 手触り: describe it; 泥臭い: name the hands-on work; 温度感: how keen they are; 熱量: say how much work went in; 等身大の: ordinary; 肌感: what you saw, or the number; 腹落ち: 納得; 血の通った: say what is personal in it; 解像度が高: understand in detail Pattern: `(?:地に足のついた)|(?:手触り)|(?:泥臭(?:い|さ))|(?:温度感)|(?:熱量)|(?:等身大の)|(?:肌感(?:覚)?)|(?:腹落ち)|(?:血の通った)|(?:解像度(?:が上が|が高|を上げ))`
- ja-thin-human-markers (weak): Almost never uses ので, ながら or ば, and seldom quotes anyone. Instead: Use the connectives you would say aloud, and quote people in 「」.
- ja-trailing-colon (weak; post, document): Ends a sentence with a colon before a list, as English does. Instead: End it with 。 Pattern: `[ぁ-ん]：\s*$`
