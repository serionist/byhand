# Signs of AI writing: Chinese, every model

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section; one phrase counts once.

- zh-aphorism (weak): Ends a point on a coined maxim, X is the Y of Z or the real X is Y, that adds no fact. Instead: Say the plain claim behind it, or cut it.
- zh-chat-residue (weak): Keeps chat lines meant for the person who asked: hope this helps, copy it as is, ask me anything else. Instead: Cut them; end with the text itself.
- zh-connective-stacking (weak; post, document): Starts sentence after sentence with 此外, 然而, 与此同时 or 因此, where the order already links them. Instead: Drop most of them; keep one only where it carries a turn or a cause.
- zh-dash-density (weak): Carries asides, turns and add-ons on dashes (——), several in one paragraph. Instead: Use a colon, a comma or a full stop; keep one dash where it really sets off an aside.
- zh-decorative-detail (weak; post, document): Invents scene details, the hour, the weather, a cold drink, that change nothing that follows. Instead: Cut them; keep a detail only if it is true and changes what happens.
- zh-era-opener (weak): Opens on a hat about the times, such as how fast some field is developing, before reaching the subject. Instead: Start with the case itself: a date, a number, a person.
- zh-first-next-last (weak): Lays any topic out in three even steps marked 首先, 其次 and 最后, whatever the logic. Instead: Follow the logic: two points if there are two, joined by what links them.
- zh-leaked-request (weak; document): Leaves the chatbot's request for input inside the text, such as asking for the passage to translate. Instead: Delete it, and check what the tool really returned there.
- zh-lyrical-abstract (weak): Dresses abstract things, such as memory, growth or an answer, in lyrical words. Instead: Say the thing plainly; keep these words for real objects. Terms: `安放`: put, or cut; `微光`: say what it is; `褶[皱皺]`: say what it is; `[丰豐]盈`: full; `[滚滾][烫燙]`: say what it is
- zh-mixed-metaphors (weak): Mixes several metaphor sets in one paragraph: race tracks, moats, waves, engines, battles. Instead: Say it literally; keep one metaphor only if it explains something.
- zh-officialese (weak): Pads a verb into officialese: 进行 plus a noun, 实现了……的提升, 起到……作用, 予以. Instead: Use the verb itself, with who did it.
- zh-significance-words (weak): Props up a plain fact with set words of significance, legacy and role. Instead: Say what happened, to whom and when; drop the word if no fact stands behind it. Terms: `标志着|標誌著`: say what changed; `见证了|見證了`: say what happened; `奠定了?(?:坚实的?|堅實的?)?基[础礎]`: say what it made possible; `不可磨[灭滅]`: say what remains; `根植[于於]`: comes from; `意[义義](?:重大|非凡)`: say what it means; `至[关關]重要|[举舉]足[轻輕]重`: say why it matters; `深[远遠]的?影[响響]`: name the effect; `扮演[着著了]?[^。，\n]{0,10}角色`: say what it does; `不[断斷](?:变化|演变|變化|演變)的格局`: name the market
- zh-social-media-register (weak; post): Piles up social-media hype words, as a post written in bulk does. Instead: Keep one if it is how you talk; say what the post gives. Terms: `保姆[级級]`: detailed; `建[议議]收藏`: cut; `一文[读讀]懂`: cut; `[划劃]重[点點]`: cut; `干[货貨]|乾貨`: cut; `[绝絕][绝絕]子|[谁誰]懂啊`: cut; `姐妹[们們]`: cut
- zh-staged-drama (weak): Stages drama: a run of one-phrase fragments, a teasing question before the point, or a canned pause before a reveal. Instead: Write ordinary sentences with a subject; keep one short line at most.
- zh-stock-openers (weak): Opens with a stock signpost that a point matters, or announces what comes next, instead of saying it. Instead: Cut the signpost and start with the point. Terms: `值得(?:注意|一提|关注|關注)的是`: cut; `需要指出的是|不可否[认認]的是|不[难難][发發][现現]`: cut; `[众眾]所周知`: cut, or name the source; `毋庸置疑|不言而喻`: cut; `让我们一起来看看|讓我們一起來看看|接下来我将为[你您]|接下來我將為[你您]`: start with it; `[废廢][话話]不多[说說]`: cut
- zh-translationese (weak): Reads like English carried into Chinese: long chains of 的, stacked 被 passives, and frames like 对于……而言 or 基于. Instead: Break the chain into short clauses with a subject, in the active voice.
- zh-uplift-ending (weak): Closes on an upbeat line about the future, or a call to embrace change together. Instead: End on the last concrete point, or the next step and its date. Terms: `拭目以待`: cut; `未[来來]可期`: cut; `[让讓]我[们們]一起(?:拥抱|擁抱|迎接|期待|携手|攜手)`: name the next step
- zh-wrap-up-phrases (weak): Restates the point under a stock wrap-up, or claims to reach its essence, adding nothing. Instead: Stop at the last new point, or state the claim without the frame. Terms: `[综綜]上所述|[总總]而言之|[总總]的[来來][说說看]`: cut; `由此可[见見]`: cut; `[归歸]根[结結][底柢]|[说說]到底`: cut; `本[质質]上`: say the claim; `(?:核心|[关關][键鍵])在[于於]`: say what it is
