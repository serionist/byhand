# Signs of AI writing: Korean, every model, to check a text

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.
A pattern is what Grep runs on a file, case-insensitive and line by line; a sign with none is found by reading.

- ko-paired-antithesis (usual): Builds paragraph after paragraph on set-up contrasts, X가 아니라 Y or twin questions X인가, Y인가. Correcting a fact (15일이 아니라 16일) is not one. Instead: Say what the thing is; set it against another only where a reader really holds that other view.
- ko-abstract-subject (weak): Gives a will or a voice to something that has none: a market that has answered, a report card that points the way. Instead: Put the people who did it in the subject.
- ko-beyond-mere (weak): Waves off a small version of the thing before naming it (단순한 A를 넘어), a frame rare in Korean before 2024. Instead: Say what it is; if both halves matter, give each plainly. Pattern: `단순한 (?:[^\s.,!?]*[^\s.,!?서고며데요다니면지게만라도는은이가] )?[^\s.,!?]+[을를] 넘어(?:서|서는|선)?(?:[\s,]|$)`
- ko-cleft-emphasis (weak): Frames the point as what is needed or what matters, then names it. Instead: Make the thing itself the subject of a plain sentence. Words: 관건은: say what decides it; 중요한 것은: ~가 필요합니다 Pattern: `\b(?:(?:관건은)|(?:(?:중요한|필요한) 것은))\b`
- ko-coined-metaphor (weak): Dresses a plain point in an image, a blueprint, a red light, a rival eating into a market, where the fact would do. Instead: Give the number or the event the image stands for. Words: 경고등…: say what is wrong; 뿌리내려…: 자리 잡다; 신호탄…: 첫 사례; 움켜줬…: say what is held; 잠식…: give the share lost; 적신호…: say what is wrong; 짓누르…: give the cost; 청사진…: 계획 Pattern: `\b(?:(?:경고등\w*)|(?:뿌리(?:내려|내렸|내리|내린|내릴)\w*)|(?:신호탄\w*)|(?:움켜(?:줬|쥐|쥐어|쥐었|쥔|쥘|쥡)\w*)|(?:잠식\w*)|(?:적신호\w*)|(?:짓(?:누르|누른|누를|눌러|눌렀|눌려|눌리|눌린)\w*)|(?:청사진\w*))\b`
- ko-comma-after-ending (weak): In sentence after sentence, a comma sits where -지만, -면서 or -는데 already joins two clauses. Instead: Delete the comma; the clauses stay joined without it. Pattern: `(?:[^\s하렇]|\S하|[^\s그]렇)지만,|(?:[^\s러]|[^\s그]러)면서,|\S(?:는데|으며|하며),`
- ko-double-hedge (weak): Stacks two hedges on one claim, so the possibility itself becomes possible. Instead: Keep one hedge at the strength you mean; in a negotiation, leave the writer's own care alone. Pattern: `가능성(?:도|이) 있을 수(?: 있|도 있)|(?:보여질|여겨질) 수(?: 있|도 있)`
- ko-em-dash (weak): Sets off asides with em dashes, sentence after sentence. Instead: Use a comma, brackets or a new sentence. Pattern: `—`
- ko-enumeration-preamble (weak): Announces how many parts a list has before giving it. Instead: Give the items. Pattern: `크게 (?:두|세|네|다섯|[2-5]) ?가지로 (?:구분|나누|나눌|나눠|나뉘|나뉜|나뉩|요약|정리)`
- ko-gemini-colon-headings (weak; gemini 2.5 and later; document, post): Breaks heading after heading at a colon, a topic on the left and a catchline on the right. Instead: Write the heading as what the section tells the reader, with no colon. Pattern: `^#{1,6}\s+[^\n:]+:\s*\S`
- ko-gemini-shift-slogan (weak; gemini 2.5 and later): Sums up a change as a slogan from one quoted label to another, 'X'에서 'Y'로. Instead: Say what changed, for whom, and when. Pattern: `['‘"“][^'’"”\n]{1,20}['’"”](?:에서|[을를] 넘어) ['‘"“][^'’"”\n]{1,20}['’"”](?:로|으로)`
- ko-gpt-2024-vocabulary (weak; gpt 4 to 4.x): Uses the verbs of 2024 GPT models in Korean: contribute, explore, emphasise, an important role. Instead: Use the plain verb, or say what was done. Words: 강조하…: say it once; 기여하…: 돕다; 중요한 역할…: say what it does; 탐구하…: 알아보다 Pattern: `\b(?:(?:강조(?:하|한|할|함|합|해|했)\w*)|(?:기여(?:하|한|할|함|합|해|했)\w*)|(?:중요한 역할\w*)|(?:탐구(?:하|한|할|함|합|해|했)\w*))\b`
- ko-gpt-meaning-wrap (weak; gpt 5 and later): Closes a summing-up sentence on 'which means that', ~다는 뜻이다. Instead: End on the claim itself, with -다. Pattern: `다는 (?:뜻|의미)(?:이다|입니다)`
- ko-heading-summary-line (weak; document): Opens a section by announcing what the section will cover. Instead: Start the section with its first fact. Pattern: `^\W*(?:본|이|이번) (?:단락|섹션|장|절)에서는`
- ko-no-long-sentences (weak; document, post): Writes only short one-clause sentences of even length, never a long one that carries an argument through. Instead: Where one thought runs into the next, let it run on in one sentence; save the short ones for a turn.
- ko-obligation-endings (weak; document, post): Ends one paragraph after another on a duty: ~할 필요가 있다, or ~해야 한다. Instead: Keep the call to act where it matters; let the other paragraphs end on their facts.
- ko-policy-verbs (weak): Lets 구축, 강화 and 확대 stand in for every action, and has 설계 shape things nobody draws up, such as a relationship. Instead: Write what was done, by whom, in its own verb: 담당자를 한 명 늘렸다. Words: 경험을 설계…: say how, in a verb Pattern: `\b(?:(?:(?:경험|관계|문화|여정|일상)[을를] (?:재)?설계\w*))\b`
- ko-register-shift-vocabulary (weak): Uses the academic verbs that spread through Korean journal abstracts from late 2024; Claude Sonnet 5 wrote them above the human rate. Instead: Use the plain verb (보여 주다, 밝히다, 다루다) or state the finding. Words: 규명…: 밝히다; 시사점…: 보여 주다; 조명하…: 다루다 Pattern: `\b(?:(?:규명\w*)|(?:시사(?:점|하|한|할|함|합|해|했)\w*)|(?:조명(?:하|한|할|함|합|해|했)\w*))\b`
- ko-scare-quotes (weak): Puts quotation marks round coined labels again and again, not round anything anyone said. Instead: Keep quotation marks for words someone actually used; write your own terms bare.
- ko-significance-phrases (weak): Props a point up with set phrases of weight and meaning. Instead: Say what follows from it, or cut the phrase. Words: 간과할 수 없…: cut; 방점을 찍…: say where the weight falls; 새로운 장을 여…: say what starts; 의미가 적지 않…: say what it means; 주목할 만하…: say why Pattern: `\b(?:(?:간과할 수 없\w*)|(?:방점을 찍\w*)|(?:새로운 장을 (?:여|연|열|엽)\w*)|(?:의미가 적지 않\w*)|(?:주목할 만(?:하|한|합)\w*))\b`
- ko-sino-abstract-chain (weak): Strings Sino-Korean abstract nouns and -적, -성, -화 words together where a verb with a subject would do. Instead: Turn the noun back into a verb and say who does it.
- ko-slogan-close (weak): Ends a paragraph on a stock line: now is the time to, that is why, it all leads to. Instead: End on the last fact, or give the actual chain of cause. Pattern: `\S+ (?:때|시점)(?:다|이다|입니다)[.!]?\s*$|[는은] 이유(?:다|이다|입니다)[.]?\s*$|(?:로|으로) 이어(?:진다|집니다|질 (?:것|수))|에 직결(?:된다|됩니다)`
- ko-wrap-up-words (weak): Labels a conclusion before giving it, or ends on an academic hedge that it can be called so. Instead: Drop the label and the hedge; give the conclusion. Pattern: `\b(?:결론적으로|요약하(?:면|자면)|종합하(?:건대|면|자면))\b|[라다]고 (?:볼|할) 수 있(?:다|습니다|을 것)|[음함]을 알 수 있(?:다|습니다)`
