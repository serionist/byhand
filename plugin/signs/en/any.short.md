# Signs of AI writing: English, every model, for every kind of writing

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- en-stock-openers (usual): Uses a stock line about the modern world, or about unlocking potential. Instead: Start with the subject. Words: in today's modern age: cut; navigate the complexity: say what the task is; unlock the power: say what becomes possible
- en-ai-vocabulary (weak): Leans on the same few elevated words; one is chance, several together are not. Instead: Use the plain word, or the fact the word stands in for. Words: align with: matches; complexities: problems; crucial: say why it matters; deep dive: close look; delve: look at; foster: build; interconnectedness: links; interplay: how A affects B; intricate: detailed; let's dive in: cut; leverage: use; multifaceted: name the parts; nuanced: say what the nuance is; pivotal: say why it matters; robust: say how it holds up; seamless: smooth; tapestry: mix; transformative: say what changed; underscored, underscore a: shows; vibrant: lively
- en-even-sentences (weak): Writes sentence after sentence of one length and one shape. Instead: Let length follow the thought, some short and some long.
- en-significance-phrases (weak): Reaches for set phrases that inflate a subject's importance. Instead: Say what happened and to whom. Words: enduring legacy: say what remains; evolving landscape: name the market or field; indelible mark: say what changed; is a testament to: shows; mark a pivotal shift: give the date and the change; play a vital role: say what it does; reflect broader: name the trend, or cut; setting the stage for: led to; valuable insights: say what was learnt
- en-stiff-verbs (weak): Picks the stiff verb where a person uses the short one. Instead: Use the short verb. Words: attempted: tried; authored: wrote; relocated: moved; utilise: use
- en-vague-connection (weak): Joins two things with a loose word of connection and never says how. Instead: Give the actual relation: who did what, and where.
