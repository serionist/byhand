# Signs of AI writing: English, GPT, for every kind of writing

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase, even two from one sign: the same one repeated, a mark such as a dash, quotes or bold however often it appears and whatever words it holds, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.
A sign holds for the versions it names: `5.x` is the whole 5 series, `and later` every newer one.

- en-copula-avoidance (weak; gpt 4 and later): Avoids plain 'is' and 'has' with grander verbs. Instead: Write 'is' or 'has'. Words: boasts a: has; functions as a: is; holds the distinction of being: is; served as a: is; stands as a: is
- en-em-dash (weak; gpt 3.5 to 5.0): Uses spaced em dashes as pivots, more than a person writing the same kind of text. Instead: Use a comma, a colon, brackets or a full stop.
- en-gpt-2023-vocabulary (weak; gpt 3.5 to 4.x): Uses the words of 2023 to mid-2024 ChatGPT. Instead: Use the plain word. Words: boasts: has; bolster: support; enduring: lasting; garner: got; intricacy: details; meticulous: careful; testament: proof
- en-gpt-2024-vocabulary (weak; gpt 4 to 5.x): Uses the words of mid-2024 to mid-2026 ChatGPT. Instead: Use the plain word. Words: emphasising: stressing; enhance: improve; highlighting: showing; showcase: shows
- en-gpt-5-vocabulary (weak; gpt 5 to 5.x): Stresses media coverage and online presence, as GPT-5 does. Instead: Say what the source says, not what kind of source it is. Words: independent coverage: name the report; local media outlets: name them; has a active online presence: cut; trade publications: name them
- en-gpt-6-vocabulary (weak; gpt 6 and later): Uses GPT-6's plainer words. Instead: Prefer the exact word. Words: dependable: reliable; echoed: say who repeated it; prioritise: put first; universally: say who
- en-nominalisation (weak; gpt 4 to 4.x): Packs sentences with nouns made from verbs. Instead: Turn the noun back into a verb, with a named subject.
