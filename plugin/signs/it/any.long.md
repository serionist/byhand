# Signs of AI writing: Italian, every model, for posts and documents

Generated by `byhand render` from the catalogue; a change here is lost.
A weak sign counts only with 3 or more hits of weak signs in a paragraph or section, each a different word or phrase: the same one repeated, or one phrase two signs find, counts once.

- it-even-sentences (weak; post): Keeps sentences close to one length and one shape, where people mix short ones with long ones. Instead: Let a short sentence follow a long one where the thought needs it.
- it-inflated-stakes (weak; post, document): Frames a plain fact as an epoch: in un mondo sempre più… Instead: Give the date, the number or the change itself. Words: in un mondo sempre più: cut
- it-staccato-fragments (weak; post, document): Strings three or more short verbless fragments in a row, each closed by a full stop, for a punchy beat. Instead: Join them into one sentence with a verb; keep a lone fragment only where it earns the stress.
